## What changed outside the released specification
The latest release is still v2026-08-25, but the project and its vendors moved:
- Google's Universal Cart launched in the US on 2026-10-15 with checkout "powered by UCP"; Google said on 2026-09-16 that UCP enables direct checkout for "hundreds of thousands of brands and retailers".
- Shopify's help pages confirm that direct checkout on Google surfaces is on by default for eligible US stores, with no fees beyond payment processing; Microsoft says it takes no commission today; Google itself still publishes no fee schedule.
- A breaking draft change to webhook signing merged on 2026-10-16 (webhook-id and webhook-timestamp join the signed set); a contribution process with capability maturity levels was published on 2026-10-12; Google's FAQ added mutual TLS as a way to verify its requests; the A2A sample is unmaintained pending a rewrite.
