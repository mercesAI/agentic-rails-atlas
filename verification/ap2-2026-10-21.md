Of the 98 findings, none was high, 27 were medium and 71 low. Every identifier the site uses exists in AP2 v0.2, and every example payload validates against the AP2 JSON Schemas. The fixers rejected two findings with evidence and deferred one to the owner; the critic found five more problems and fixed them.

## What the round found
- FIDO set a timeline on 2026-09-08: harmonized AP2 and Verifiable Intent specifications targeted for the end of 2026 and a first consolidated version for mid-2027. The site said FIDO gave no date, and that its drafts are member-only, which no source supports. No public FIDO draft was found as of 2026-10-21.
- Google Cloud and PayPal announced an AP2-aligned PayPal agent on 2025-10-27; the site had no record of it. It is an announcement, not production use.
- Two papers analyze AP2 v0.2 directly: arXiv 2607.21824 (14 vulnerabilities), which the site had attributed to OpenAI ACP and UCP, and arXiv 2610.21063, whose five applicable approval-path attacks AP2 rejected or partly resisted.
- Several mechanics were stated too broadly (checkout hashes, optional iat and exp, how open mandates end, liability), and ERC-8226 is identity-agnostic rather than built on ERC-8004.
- AP2's repository has merged nothing since 2026-04-29; 65 pull requests and 47 issues were opened since.
