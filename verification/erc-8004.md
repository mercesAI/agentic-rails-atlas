### asOf rule (Check 1, literal)

The brief fails a claim that carries a number or date without `asOf`. In the checked set, 12 claims state an explicit date and have no `asOf`: 003 004 005 006 007 090 091 092 101 102 121 219. All dates are correct (fixed historical events). Fix: add `asOf` (the date of the record read, for example 2026-10-11) to every dated claim, or record in `DECISIONS.md` that immutable event dates are exempt. Across all 220 claims the same heuristic finds 63 without `asOf` (most are spec constants such as `uint8` or "0 to 18").
