## What changed outside the site
- 2026-06-15 (older item): Akamai announced an agentic security framework for its Bot & Agent Control products. It says frameworks like TAP help define how agents operate in payment environments, but names no TAP-specific setting and does not mention the Agentic Directory, Visa Intelligent Commerce or Web Bot Auth (claim 266; the Akamai row and the timeline).
- 2026-07-24 (older item): a Visa Worldwide release on PR Newswire announced a live business-to-business purchase by LoopXPay, Lianlian DigiTech's agent, registered in the Agentic Directory, which the release says supports the implementation of TAP. - 2026-11-17 (Business Wire dateline; the usa.visa.com page shows 2026-11-18): Visa extended Agentic Ready to merchants, business-to-business suppliers, payment service providers, acquirers and commerce platforms, launching with more than 140 participants that test readiness, beside 175+ participating issuers in 45 markets. Its four-stage framework asks merchants to configure bot management to recognize trusted agent traffic and register endpoints in the Agentic Directory, and payment providers to process tokenized credentials and ensure cryptogram submission; it never names TAP, and the FAQ calls the program protocol-agnostic. The release names TAP only for Airwallex; only the Perspectives copy adds J.P. Morgan Payments, testing how TAP signals travel with agentic network tokens. PayPal is working to expand acceptance of Visa agentic tokens across its merchants through their existing PayPal integration. On 2026-11-18 the European release named more than 85 acceptance participants and more than 40 issuers, and the Asia Pacific release 50+ participants and over 100 issuers in 10 markets.
