## What changed outside the site
- 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15 (UTC): a third, smaller Central City wave ran on Optimism. The registry's highest agentId rose from 780 (2026-11-14T00:00Z) to 22,083 (2026-11-16T00:00Z), reached 25,901 on 2026-11-18 and, after a second, smaller rise from 2026-11-20, 30,484 at 2026-11-21T07:05Z; three random samples of ids point to Central City, each id with a different owner, and a spot check found one Olas marketplace agent (id 22,084). Central City registrations continued on Base (highest id 1,186,989 to 1,219,893 between the reads of 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-21, more ids than Optimism added over that interval) and on Arc (417,132 to 429,353).
- 2026-11-16: mppx 0.20.0 (wevm/mppx PR #1304) shipped an ERC-8004 agent identity provider. The agent signs each request with its wallet (EIP-191) and sends Agent-Id and Agent-Signature headers; the server reads getAgentWallet from the Identity Registry, by default on Tempo mainnet (chain 4217, not in the contracts README), and also counts a tempo charge paid from that wallet as proof, while sessions, Zones and transaction hashes fall back to the request signature. A signature is valid for 300 seconds plus 60 seconds of clock tolerance and is single-use only with a replay store. It is an SDK feature, not part of ERC-8004 or of any MPP draft, and the payment discovery draft still only cites ERC-8004. MPP's identity playground added an ERC-8004 sample (2026-11-17) and a shared MPP Demo Agent, agent 1086 on the Moderato testnet (2026-11-18); PR #1332 (opened 2026-11-19, open on 2026-11-21) would bind verification to a configured origin, and mppx 0.21.0 (2026-11-19) added optional host allowlists.
- 2026-11-17: Kite's docs changelog says Kite Chain is no longer a Passport chain, so Passport refuses deposits, payments and transfers on it (written into the Passport skills the same day by commit 85e2a49, and repeated in the entry of 2026-11-18). Undated, seen 2026-11-21: Kite's service provider guide has Agent Passport pay x402 services with the standard exact scheme through any x402-compatible facilitator (its sample is still in the V1 wire format), lists the chains a service settles on (Base, Polygon, Avalanche, Tempo or Arc in USDC, Solana in USDC or PYUSD, Robinhood Chain in USDG) and no longer mentions gokite-aa or Pieverse.
- 2026-11-17: ERC-8048 (PR #2181) and ERC-8049 (PR #2182), both still Draft, now require their agent profile keys (context, endpoints, addresses and accounts) to be stored on-chain and kept out of tokenURI or contractURI JSON (ERC-8048 tells clients to ignore them there), because JSON can change without an on-chain event. ERC-8122 keeps all its agent metadata on-chain through ERC-8048. Before the window, on 2026-11-11, the same author closed his draft ERC-8156 for ERC-8004 registries as no longer needed.

No status changed.
- 2026-11-20: ERC-8226 was rewritten (PR #2191, commit 9912307). A mandate is keyed by agent, principal and target contract, with allowed actions, calldata constraints and per-asset OUTFLOW and INFLOW limits in place of the single caps; operators the principal approves may grant, revoke and extend mandates; enforcers freeze and may remove an operator but cannot revoke; IAgentExecutor moved into the specification as an optional interface, and the account venue became one shared, stateless EIP-7702 delegate deployment per chain and registry, with a smart-account module as an alternative. It is still a Draft for tokenized regulated assets. The author announced the rewrite in the Ethereum Magicians thread (57 posts by then) and posted the authors' own test deployments on Ethereum Sepolia and Base Sepolia.
