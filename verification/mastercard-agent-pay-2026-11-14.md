- 2026-10-29: Mastercard's CEO said on the Q3 2026 call that Shop Pay uses Agent Pay and Mastercard network tokens, and that a broad strategic partnership with OpenAI signed that quarter includes the use of Agent Pay. He described the early stages of consumer adoption, with numbers not yet significant (third-party transcript, reported). The written Q3 materials (release, presentation, supplemental data, Form 10-Q) give no agentic figure and no US enablement update. OpenAI's merchant guidance (read 2026-11-14) says ChatGPT's cloud browser signs certain requests with `Signature-Agent: "https://chatgpt.com"`, verified by Akamai, Cloudflare, HUMAN and Vercel, and does not state the tag.
- 2026-11-12: Travelport, Booked AI, Basis Theory and Mastercard announced a live end-to-end flight purchase in which, they say, Agent Pay enabled the payment with a tokenized credential and Verifiable Intent linked the customer's approval to the transaction. The release names no airline, Verifiable Intent layer or checking party; Mastercard's own copy answered 403.
