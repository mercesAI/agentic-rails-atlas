## What changed at Visa
Between 2026-10-13 and 2026-10-19 Visa moved its developer documentation to /docs/. The old specification, overview and terms URLs now redirect to a Getting started page; the overview was retired, and the Visa Intelligent Commerce documentation sits behind a sign-in. The moved specification no longer names a key URL; its text is otherwise unchanged.

## Corrections
Rows F37 to F42 in `ATLAS_ERRATA.md`. The most important:
- The key set at mcp.visa.com is what the specification named until October 2026; Visa's own code uses it to encrypt tokens for its MCP server, so the site no longer presents it as a TAP agent-key store (F37).
- Visa calls none of its agent rules a liability shift (its EMV rules do use the term), and every "no liability shift" statement is attributed to Worldpay with its hedge (F38, F39).
- The MPP card draft date (F40), the Visa Intelligent Commerce sequence and host (F41), and smaller corrections (F42).
