## What changed outside the site
UCP itself did not move: no release, tag or release-branch change, and `main` gained only six docs and CI commits (b51a8fa to c20aa35, the last merged 2026-11-21 UTC). What moved were Google's deployments, the tooling and the governance:
- 2026-11-13: the Governing Council approved opening nominations for founding Local Services and B2B Procurement Tech Councils (charter issues 922 and 947), agreed to look more closely at where PAP and UCP overlap to avoid fragmentation, and agreed in principle to regional Domain Working Groups. The minutes, published 2026-11-16, do not expand PAP; the page reads it as the Personal Agent Protocol and says so. The nomination calls (discussions 1093 and 1094, stamped 2026-11-14T05:08Z; ucp.dev dates them 2026-11-13) close 2026-11-27, with members tentatively announced 2026-12-11. The Governing Council roster is unchanged; the Shopping Tech Council replaced its Microsoft and Google representatives (#93 and #95, 2026-11-17 to 2026-11-19).
- 2026-11-17: ucp-schema 2.0.0 reached crates.io (GitHub release v2.0.0 from 66d78e2; its changelog heading is dated 2026-11-13). It is a breaking API release that removes ComposeError::UnknownParent, now reported as ComposeError::OrphanExtension, and adds type generation and profile-driven OpenAPI 3.1 generation; it is a tool release, not a protocol version. By 2026-11-19 the Python and JavaScript SDKs pinned it in a new `ucp-lock.json` on `main` and added release workflows for date-based versions (YYYYMMDD.MINOR.PATCH, for example 20260825.0.0). None is published: npm @ucp-js/sdk 0.5.1 and PyPI ucp-sdk 0.5.0 are still the latest.
- 2026-11-17: Google said Gemini now uses AP2 for purchases made with its new Google Pay checkout in Tasks in Gemini, and that it will integrate UCP into Gemini next. Google's UCP FAQ (last updated 2026-11-03) already says a merchant's UCP integration powers the Gemini apps, the post describes no UCP flow, and Google's UCP platform profiles (the .well-known profile declares 2026-04-08; versioned profiles serve 2026-01-23, 2026-04-08 and 2026-08-25) declare no AP2 extension. The AP2 report has the details. On the UCP page, live use of the AP2 extension stays unverified, and claim 197, which had said "inside UCP, or anywhere else" as of 2026-10-20, now covers UCP only.
- 2026-11-20: Google began rolling out grocery Connected Apps in the US through Search and the Gemini app, including Albertsons, Safeway, Shaw's and Target, which it says are powered by UCP. Google's Grocery ordering guide (last updated 2026-11-17; listed under October 2026 in Google's changelog) describes a sample integration for UCP 2026-08-25: location and catalog search, a cart built over UCP, and a `continue_url` from create_cart, update_cart and get_cart that sends the buyer to the retailer's own checkout for the delivery details and payment. It recommends MCP over REST for Connected Apps, assumes OAuth account linking, makes the checkout and order scopes optional, marks `buyer_address_locations` as a Google vendor extension and calls `Request-Id` optional, which the REST OpenAPI requires. Google does not say which integration each retailer runs. Google's MCP transport guide still makes REST the default for native checkout and cart.
- 2026-11-20: UCP opened nominations for three eight-week regional Domain Working Groups (APAC, EMEA and LATAM; discussion 1120, charter issues 1117 to 1119), sponsored by the Shopping and Payments Tech Councils; the call gives no deadline and names no members.
- In the window but not on the site (deferred as minor): on 2026-11-19 the organization contributing guide (commit 147641f) said vendor capabilities, extensions, services and payment handlers are self-hosted under the vendor's namespace and need no pull request to the core repository, which extends the self-hosting rule of the 2026-10-12 ucp.dev guide. On 2026-11-20 maintainers closed PRs #713 (a generic SPT payment handler) and #540 (an AP2 mandate-evaluation extension) unmerged, and commit c20aa35 (merged 2026-11-21 UTC) added Stripe's `com.stripe.payments` handler to the ucp.dev draft playground, which is documentation, not a handler that UCP ships. On 2026-11-18 Google's Demand Gen guidance listed UCP-enabled checkout as a way to shorten the path to purchase on YouTube, naming no countries or merchants. The W3C and GS1 e-commerce workshop now has a draft report (first committed 2026-11-10, last modified 2026-11-17 on w3.org) that credits UCP with around thirty organizations in its councils by September 2026. The Payments Tech Council minutes of 2026-11-05 (published 2026-11-19) record a buy now, pay later working group charter ready for Governing Council review and an off-site payment handler proposal (draft PR 1075).
