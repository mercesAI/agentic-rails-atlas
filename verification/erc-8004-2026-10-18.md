## Corrections
Each correction is a row in `ATLAS_ERRATA.md`, section "Found during the 2026-10-18 fact check" (F1 to F25). The most important:
- The mainnet listing history: fifteen mainnets were listed in eight commits (four of them pull requests) between 2026-02-02 and 2026-02-12, not twenty at once on 2026-03-06 (F1).
- Visa's public rules, which govern Visa and its member institutions and yield to applicable law, have carried agent-specific requirements since 2025-10-18: an Agentic Payment Provider must enroll in Visa Intelligent Commerce and register, and within Visa's system the cardholder is responsible for its actions. Visa calls none of these rules a liability shift. Corrected on every page that denied them (F18).
- The playground chain id 424242 was not fake; the playgrounds now use 402402 (F2).
- Precision fixes to core facts: the one payable function, the required `agentId`, the Agent role in ERC-3643, ERC-8122 as an alternative registry, the scope of ERC-8226, the upgrade key per chain, and the authorship of the MPP discovery draft (F3 to F7, F22, F25).
- Compliance rows rewritten from the primary legal texts so that none assigns a duty more broadly than the law does (F19, F20).
