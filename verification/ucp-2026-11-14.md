## What changed outside the site
The latest dated release is still v2026-08-25 (as of 2026-11-14), but its branch, its tags and the deployments around it moved:
- Between 2026-10-19 and 2026-11-03: Shopify's Google channel page opened direct checkout to stores based in any country that sell to customers in the US, Canada or Australia. Its Help Center also lists Meta (an AI channel since 2026-09-08) and treats ChatGPT as a discovery channel, and its Fall '26 Edition (2026-10-28) put Shop Pay inside personal AI agents, with Mastercard, Visa and Discover tokens, and American Express coming soon, for agent purchases from merchants off Shopify; Shopify names no network program.
- Between 2026-10-20 and 2026-10-30: Google rewrote its UCP FAQ (last updated 2026-11-03), and its network guide names `Google-Shopping-Merchant-Integration` the default User-Agent. For verification the FAQ keeps mutual TLS and adds ES256 RFC 9421 signatures against the keys at ucp.goog and source-ASN rules; it no longer offers reverse DNS, which the 2026-10-16 version listed beside published IP ranges. The network guide adds that Google's crawler and fetcher IP files must not be used for UCP and that Google does not guarantee static egress IP ranges.
- 2026-10-22 or 2026-10-23: the v2026-08-25 tag moved from its original cut cd78fb3 to 87dd4b1 (backports and rich media variants), and the v2026-04-08 tag to 9c9d6fc, which now carries permalink and Web Bot Auth interop (backported to that branch on 2026-09-30).
- 2026-10-24: Visa's rules and US interchange schedule moved to the 2026-10-24 edition; the schedule, like the April 2026 one the compliance row cited, has no agentic category.
- 2026-11-02: after Shopping Tech Council approval on 2026-10-30, PR 1024 backported Ask (`dev.ucp.common.ask`) into the 2026-08-25 release branch; ucp.dev serves it as 2026-08-25, but the git tag does not contain it. Shopify said on 2026-11-06 that Ask is live across its storefronts.
- 2026-11-04: Google began rolling out UCP direct checkout to Canadian shoppers on AI Mode and the Gemini app, the first launch outside the US in Google's posts; Universal Cart stays US-only.
- Between 2026-11-04 and about 2026-11-06: Microsoft's Copilot Checkout page dropped both PayPal and its answer that Microsoft takes no commission today; it now names Shopify and Stripe as partners, where the 2026-01-08 launch named PayPal, Shopify and Stripe.
- 2026-11-10 and 2026-11-13: the draft Food Checkout capability (`dev.ucp.food.checkout` with fulfillment and tip extensions) merged on main and was announced; it is in no release.
- 2026-11-12 to 2026-11-13: OpenAI rewrote its commerce docs into the Commerce in ChatGPT playbook; its Build a plugin guide shows a reference checkout with tool and field names that follow UCP's checkout schema rather than ACP's, naming neither protocol. On 2026-11-13 Sierra released Personal Agent Protocol 1.0, a standard from Sierra and Meta whose UCP extension Shopify co-authored, per Sierra.
- Planned, not yet true: the next UCP release is milestoned for 2026-12-08, and two new councils (Local Services, B2B Procurement) take nominations until 2026-11-27.
