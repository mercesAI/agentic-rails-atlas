- Counts on 2026-11-14: 8004scan reported 2,062,896 agents, 885,869 feedback entries and 3,341,787 unique wallets (about 1.6 wallets per agent); 0G's Identity Registry, which 8004scan does not index, reached agentId 3,941,206 (most ids from one platform by sample), so registrations across the chains exceed 6 million; Central City claims 1,478,269 agents on Base, Arc and Optimism (vendor claim).
- 2026-10-29: ERC-8217 was rewritten (ethereum/ERCs pull request #2127): it binds an ERC-8004 agent to a token, an account or a contract, requires ERC-7930 as well as ERC-8004, and adds bindingHashOf, a 32-byte Universal Binding Identifier; it is still Draft.
