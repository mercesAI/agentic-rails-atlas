## What changed outside the site
- Security: on 2026-11-18 the maintainers published a third repository advisory, GHSA-5cgh-j82v-jvrj (medium, CVSS 3.1 5.4, CWE-347, no CVE id). The batch-settlement voucher checks in the TypeScript, Go and Python SDKs accepted ECDSA signatures that the x402BatchSettlement contract cannot recover (a high s value, or v of 0 or 1), so a payer could be served, withdraw the escrow after the withdrawal delay and leave the seller with a voucher whose claim reverts. Affected are @x402/evm 2.12.0 to 2.31.0, PyPI x402 2.11.0 to 2.28.0 and the Go module 2.11.0 to 2.31.0; the advisory says ERC-1271 payers are unaffected and use of the scheme is low. PRs #4316 (2026-11-18) and #4319 (2026-11-19) carry the fixes, and the changelogs put both in the 2026-11-20 releases, but on 2026-11-21 the advisory listed no patched version and neither the GitHub Advisory Database nor OSV had an entry. CVE-2026-113603's report and fix (issue #3838, PR #3840) and the V1 paywall bypass (issue #3850) were still open on 2026-11-21, and other hardening in the window shipped without an advisory: non-canonical exact EVM amounts (#4291, #4303, #4304), a SIWE checksum fix (#4272) and the Python signer nonce (#4313).
- Releases: on 2026-11-20 all twelve @x402 packages the page lists reached 2.32.0, with PyPI x402 2.29.0 and the Go module v2.32.0 (tag go/v2.32.0 at e35bf88; go.mod still requires Go 1.26.10). Besides the voucher fixes they reject non-canonical exact EVM amounts with `invalid_exact_evm_required_amount`, send the spec code for a recipient mismatch in TypeScript and Go (PR #4174, merged 2026-11-17), add default assets and change the Go SVM batch-settlement facilitator configuration in a breaking way. The legacy V1 packages stayed at 1.2.0 (x402-axios 1.2.1), none marked deprecated.
- Specification on main, still v2.0 (x402Version 2): two batch-settlement bindings changed on 2026-11-19. On EVM, verification rule 6 accepts ECDSA signatures only in the contract's form (65 bytes, a low s value, v of 27 or 28) for voucher, refund-authorizer and claim-authorizer signatures (PR #4319). On Solana, the idempotency rewrite (PR #4222) has a facilitator reject a concurrent duplicate settle with `duplicate_settlement` instead of coalescing it, allows retries only for `settlement_pending`, adds an optional `extra.channelStates[]` and forbids falling back to an implicit public RPC. The auth-capture card binding moved only on its feature branch (PR #4299), and draft PR #4241 to main was still open. The docs added the Radius facilitator (PR #4341, 2026-11-20) and more Celo facilitator assets (PR #4011, 2026-11-17), and the SDK defaults gained Circle USDC on eight more chains, World Chain USDC in TypeScript and Python and Sperax USD on Arbitrum (2026-11-18 to 2026-11-20).
- AWS: on 2026-11-19 AWS Marketplace began selling x402 products per request in US dollars on the buyer's AWS bill through Amazon Bedrock AgentCore payments, over AWS's own `aws:base` network with asset `iso4217:USD`, outside the x402 specification. AgentCore checks the session budget and issues a payment token valid for 5 minutes, and Marketplace verifies the seller's signed quote, bills the buyer and pays the seller; only the exact scheme is supported at launch, AgentCore's guide limits one AWS Billing payment to above $0.00 and at most $10,000.00, the 3 percent SaaS listing fee applies and refunds are invoice adjustments. Named sellers are Tavily (AWS's blog) and Deepgram (its own sample, 2026-11-20), and AWS's Apache-2.0 Fiat Extension for x402 reached 1.0.2 on 2026-11-18. AWS labels the feature neither preview nor generally available, and its sources disagree on Region scope.
- Coinbase: awal 2.12.3 (2026-11-20) ships a notice that Agentic Wallet and Payments MCP are being retired in favor of Wallet MCP, a different wallet, with a withdraw-only phase that would block x402 payments; the flag endpoint the CLI reads answered 404 on 2026-11-21, so the notice was off. The former Agentic Wallet docs, which answered 404 on 2026-11-14, now redirect (HTTP 308) to the Wallet MCP page. No announcement was found, the CDP changelog's newest entry (2026-11-17) is about key export, and @coinbase/payments-mcp is still 1.0.5 and not deprecated. The Wallet MCP page (dated 2026-11-20 in CDP's sitemap) added an eligible agent-wallet mode with a US dollar budget over a rolling 24-hour window, and its x402 table now covers Base, Polygon, Arbitrum and, in agent-wallet mode only, Solana.
- Tempo: Tempo has run an x402 facilitator at api.tempo.xyz/v1/x402 since 2026-11-19 (R13: mppx 0.21.1 exports tempoX402 and the public /supported route answers; the docs pull request merged on 2026-11-18 while its author asked to wait for production). Buyers pay USDC on Base with the exact scheme to Tempo's deposit address, and Tempo pays the seller OUSD on Tempo. No announcement was found through 2026-11-19, and Tempo's own docs disagree on fees and payout timing, so the page states neither.
- Volume: at Breakpoint in London on 2026-11-17 the Solana Foundation's Rishin Sharma was reported by Decrypt and Crypto Briefing as saying x402 had processed more than 400 million payments and $100 million since launch. The figure is self-reported, no recording or Foundation post was read, and no readable source gives a Solana share.
- Research: arXiv:2611.25821 (2026-11-19) and arXiv:2611.24989 (2026-11-18), both by Shengwei You, Aditya Joshi, Andrey Kuehlkamp and Jarek Nabrzyski, describe the authority gap between agent commerce mandates and wallet signatures (98.7 percent of 26,653 sampled USDC authorizations redeemed on Base could be redeemed by any holder of the signature) and a wallet-side checker, Closure, tried in two local checkouts with the x402 reference client; they report measurements and designs, not an exploit. The publications list also tags Bajillion (arXiv:2611.21883) and Anduin (arXiv:2611.05586) with x402, and Circle's blog of 2026-11-13 says Anduin was tested on 66 million x402 payments.
- Standards and policy: at IETF 127 the HTTP working group heard draft-nottingham-httpbis-402-00 on 2026-11-20; a show of hands on interest in adopting it gave 15 yes, 3 no and 18 no opinion (75 present), which is not adoption, and the minutes were not posted on 2026-11-21. Two GENIUS Act final rules (the FDIC's, and FinCEN's with OFAC) reached White House review on 2026-11-18 and were not published by 2026-11-21. MAS and the Future of Finance Institute announced an Agentic Money Sandbox on 2026-11-18, and on 2026-11-19 MAS's managing director described its payments use case (customer agents paying with digital money such as tokenized deposits or stablecoins) and a plan for a know-your-agent protocol.
- Found this round, from before the window, and now on the site at their own dates (R3): Pay.sh (2026-05-05, reported: a Solana gateway that sells APIs over x402 and MPP, with Google Cloud per Decrypt and The Block), AgentCore payments in preview (2026-05-07, x402 only) and generally available (2026-08-18, adding MPP and the x402 upto scheme), and AWS WAF AI traffic monetization (2026-06-15: x402 at the CloudFront edge, settled through Coinbase's facilitator; Stripe and MPP were announced as coming soon, and the WAF guide read 2026-11-21 names only x402).
