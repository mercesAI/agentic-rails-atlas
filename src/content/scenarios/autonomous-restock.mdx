## The situation

A cafe owner wants coffee beans reordered whenever stock runs low, from one of two roasters, at most once a week, without approving each order. The owner is not present when the agent buys. AP2 v0.2 covers this with its human-not-present mode: the user signs open Checkout and Payment Mandates that include the agent's public key in `cnf`, and the agent later signs closed mandates with its own key.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-091 clm-ap2-092" />

This scenario is specified, not observed. Production use of AP2 mandates is unverified anywhere, including inside UCP, as of 2026-10-21.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-139" /> One third party self-reported a merchant-side AP2 v0.2 deployment in a GitHub issue on 2026-08-29; nobody has verified it.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-140" /> No commit has reached the AP2 main branch since 2026-04-29, and specification work has moved to FIDO.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-029 clm-ap2-022" />

## Actors

AP2 v0.2 defines five roles: Shopping Agent, Credential Provider, Merchant, Merchant Payment Processor and Trusted Surface.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-039" /> Here "MPP" means Merchant Payment Processor, a role unrelated to the Machine Payments Protocol.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-040" />

- **Cafe owner**, present only when the open mandates are signed.
- **Trusted Surface**, which must be non-agentic.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-041" />
- **Shopping Agent**, which is expected to be agentic, holding the key named in `cnf`.
- **Supplier**, the merchant, which signs the checkout.
- **Credential Provider**, for example a digital wallet or a payment network.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-049" />
- **Merchant Payment Processor**, the supplier's processor.

## Step by step

All mandate steps belong to AP2 v0.2. Catalog and checkout calls belong to the surrounding commerce protocol, which AP2 leaves open and designs to be compatible with UCP.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-104" />

1. **Task (no protocol).** The owner states the task and the limits.
2. **Propose open mandates.** The open Checkout Mandate requires `vct` (`mandate.checkout.open.1`), `constraints` and `cnf`, and its constraints must include `checkout.line_items`.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-055 clm-ap2-056" /> The open Payment Mandate requires `vct` (`mandate.payment.open.1`), `constraints` and `cnf`, and must include `payment.reference`.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-064" /> Here the agent adds `checkout.allowed_merchants`, `payment.amount_range`, `payment.agent_recurrence` with `WEEKLY`, `payment.budget` and `payment.allowed_payees`.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-066 clm-ap2-067 clm-ap2-070" />
3. **Consent.** The Trusted Surface shows the limits and authenticates the owner.
4. **Sign and leave.** The Trusted Surface signs the open mandates with the user key. AP2 recommends the smallest `exp` that still lets the agent finish.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-094" />
5. **Shop (commerce protocol).** When stock is low, the agent builds a cart with a supplier.
6. **Merchant-signed checkout.** The supplier returns `checkout_jwt`, a merchant-signed JWT of the checkout.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-052" />
7. **Pre-check.** All validation must run in deterministic code.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-042" /> The agent must not present a new closed mandate from the same open mandate until it receives a rejection receipt for the previous one.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-093" />
8. **Close the mandates with key binding.** The closed mandate is a key-binding JWT signed with the agent key named in `cnf`, and its `sd_hash` covers the user-signed open mandate it extends.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-180" /> `transaction_id` in the Payment Mandate is the hash of `checkout_jwt`, which ties payment to this checkout.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-060" />
9. **Present the payment chain.** The agent presents only the open-mandate disclosures needed to evaluate the closed mandates.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-186" />
10. **Credential Provider verifies.** The verifier processes the chain, checks that the closed mandate keeps every value the open mandate fixed, and evaluates each constraint.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-202" /> The budget check adds the requested amount to all earlier closed amounts.<Cite ids="clm-scn-autonomous-restock-001" /> The token is released only after the final Payment Mandate verifies.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-122" />
11. **Send the checkout chain.** The agent sends the token and the open and closed Checkout Mandates to the supplier.
12. **Merchant verifies.** The supplier checks that the hash of its checkout JWT matches `checkout_hash` and evaluates every open constraint.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-201" /> A line item requirement must be matched without reusing an item.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-073" />
13. **Initiate payment.** The supplier passes the token and both hashes to its processor.
14. **Processor verifies; Payment Receipt.** The processor must verify that the credential is scoped to the checkout, for example by finding the closed Payment Mandate inside it.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-200" /> The Payment Receipt requires `status`, `iss`, `iat`, `reference` and `payment_id`, plus `psp_confirmation_id` and `network_confirmation_id` on success.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-079" />
15. **Checkout Receipt.** It requires `status`, `iss`, `iat` and `reference`, plus `order_id` on success.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-080" /> A receipt's `reference` is the hash of the closed mandate, computed like `sd_hash`.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-081" />
16. **Store and narrow.** After a success receipt the agent stores the open mandate, closed mandate and receipt together and narrows the open mandate's scope.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-103" />

## Which protocol does what

| Steps | Protocol | Object or message | Spec version |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 to 4 | AP2 | open mandates, `cnf`, constraints | v0.2 |
| 5 | Commerce protocol (for example UCP) | cart and checkout calls | outside AP2 |
| 6 to 10 | AP2 | `checkout_jwt`, closed mandates (`sd_hash`, `transaction_id`), Credential Provider verification | v0.2 |
| 11 to 16 | AP2 | merchant and processor verification, Checkout and Payment Receipts | v0.2 |

AP2 v0.2 was released on 2026-04-28.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-027" /> Each mandate type carries a `vct` with a numeric suffix that verifiers must match exactly.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-034" />

## Where controls apply

- **Before authorization.** Native caps are `payment.amount_range` and `payment.budget`; native scoping is `checkout.allowed_merchants` and `payment.allowed_payees`.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-127 clm-ap2-128" /> A Trusted Surface template can refuse wide constraints.<Cite ids="src-ap2-schema-open-payment" />
- **At signing.** A deterministic signer that holds the agent key, separate from the language model, can refuse overlapping or out-of-budget closed mandates. AP2 requires the rejection receipts it relies on to be integrity protected from the agent's model.<Cite ids="clm-scn-autonomous-restock-003" />
- **At the edge.** The supplier's verification runs before any charge, and an unknown constraint type always fails.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-074" />
- **At settlement.** Recurrence and budget checks need the verifier to track earlier presentations.<Cite ids="clm-scn-autonomous-restock-002 clm-scn-autonomous-restock-001" /> Credential Providers, networks or processors may reject overlapping mandates or invalidate earlier tokens.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-130" />
- **After the fact.** Dispute verification recomputes the `checkout_jwt` hash, checks both receipt references against the closed mandates and verifies both mandates.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-097" /> AP2 leaves retention, retrieval and adjudication outside the specification.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-098" />

## What is observable from outside

Almost nothing. AP2 defines no public registry, explorer, indexer, event stream or `.well-known` path of its own.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-131" /> Mandates and receipts travel only among the agent, Credential Provider, merchant, processor and network.<Cite ids="src-ap2-spec-v02" /> SD-JWT digests are salted and decoys are allowed, so even a verifier sees only the constraints it is shown.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-123" /> From outside, a supplier that runs AP2 through UCP is visible only through its profile, which lists the AP2 capability.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-106" /> The card authorization looks like any other card payment.

## What can go wrong

- **A constraint fails.** The verifier answers `unresolved_constraint`, which may signal a fallback, while `invalid_credential` and `invalid_mandate` are terminal.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-082 clm-ap2-083" /> A failing check must come back as a signed receipt carrying the error.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-182" />
- **The owner is needed after all.** A human-not-present flow can turn into a human-present one when a verifier returns `unresolved_constraint` and the user approves closed mandates.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-096" /> There is no step-up in v0.2.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-118" />
- **Double spend.** The rejection-receipt rule exists so one open mandate cannot approve two checkouts at once.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-093" />
- **Leaked agent key.** AP2 v0.2 defines no way to revoke a mandate before it expires.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-117" /> The short `exp` and the budget are the only bounds.
- **Ambiguous budget.** `payment.budget` types `max` as a number with no stated unit, and the documentation example uses 1000.00, so the unit is unresolved in v0.2.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-193" /> A verifier and an agent that read it differently will disagree.
- **Prompt injection.** AP2 assumes it cannot be prevented and treats every model and agent as a potential attacker.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-116" />
- **Who pays for a bad order.** AP2 leaves liability to the payment networks.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-120" />

## Variants

- **Monthly instead of weekly.** `payment.agent_recurrence` takes `MONTHLY` or other frequencies with an optional `max_occurrences`.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-070" /> Note that the checkout side has only `checkout.allowed_merchants` and `checkout.line_items`, and one open mandate's line items cannot be split across several checkouts, so how one open Checkout Mandate serves repeated orders is not spelled out in v0.2.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-066 clm-ap2-192" />
- **Agent provider signs instead of the user.** In the Trusted Agent Provider model the provider signs mandates with a key the agent cannot use without the Trusted Surface, and verifiers trust a list of providers instead of a user credential issuer.<Cite ids="clm-ap2-047 clm-ap2-204" />
