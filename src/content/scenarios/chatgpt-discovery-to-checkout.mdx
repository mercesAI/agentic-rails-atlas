## The situation

A shopper asks ChatGPT for a pair of trail running shoes, picks a pair from a retailer and buys them. The shopper is present and approves the payment. The retailer sells and charges the card through its own checkout, and OpenAI's guide says the business handles the order, fulfillment, customer support and fraud decisions.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-004" />

What ChatGPT does in this journey changed twice in 2026. On 2026-03-24 OpenAI expanded the Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP) to product discovery and let merchants use their own checkout while it focused on discovery.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-178 clm-openai-acp-177" /> On 2026-11-13 it introduced [ChatGPT Wallet](/orbit/chatgpt-wallet/) and began rolling it out in dots, its cloud agents, for ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US aged 18 and older with eligible US-issued cards.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-001 clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-025" /> As read 2026-11-21, the post names American Express, Chase, Mastercard and Visa as partners; its launch-day text named American Express, Mastercard and Visa only.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-001" /> In the same days OpenAI replaced its Agentic Commerce developer docs with the Commerce in ChatGPT partner playbook, which covers feeds, browser access, Wallet payments and plugins and no longer describes ACP, Instant Checkout or delegated payment.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-018" /> OpenAI's merchant page still says ChatGPT shopping is built on ACP, which supports discovery today and will expand to the full shopping journey.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-017" />

As of 2026-11-21 the purchase can take three paths, and they differ in status. **Path A**, live in a limited rollout: the shopper's dot uses its cloud browser on the merchant's website and pays with a Wallet token in the existing payment form.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-004 clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-011" /> **Path B**: a merchant plugin built as a headless Model Context Protocol (MCP) server prepares the cart and checkout and hands the rest to the website with a `continue_url`; external checkout is the generally available plugin path.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-019 clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-020 clm-openai-acp-183" /> **Path C**, a beta: the ChatGPT payment sheet, which OpenAI's plugin guidelines call Instant Checkout, open only to select marketplace partners.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-021 clm-openai-acp-184" />

## Actors

- **Shopper**, who asks, compares and approves each purchase. Path A needs a dot, ChatGPT Pro in the US and an eligible US-issued card with a US billing address, and the shopper approves each purchase with biometric verification on a supported device.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-001 clm-openai-acp-292 clm-openai-acp-293" />
- **ChatGPT**, which finds products, shows the Wallet approval and, in path C, the payment sheet.
- **The shopper's dot**, an always-on agent that lives in the cloud with its own computer and browser.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-025" /> Its cloud browser visits the merchant's site on the shopper's behalf and signs certain requests with Web Bot Auth.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-008" />
- **Merchant**, which claims its business profile and uploads a product feed in Business Hub, runs the website checkout and, optionally, a plugin.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-015 clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-019" />
- **Merchant's content delivery network (CDN) or firewall**, which verifies the signatures; OpenAI says Akamai, Cloudflare, HUMAN and Vercel do so automatically.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-009" />
- **Card network and card issuer**, behind the payment token. OpenAI's post says the payment network provides the token after the shopper approves, and the Wallet terms let OpenAI ask a payment service provider, card network or issuer for the credential.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-003" /> The eligible cards are US-issued American Express, Chase, Mastercard and Visa cards, and only the primary cardholder can add a Chase card, approving the connection in the Chase app.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-292" />
- **Merchant payment service provider (PSP) and risk provider**, which authorize the token and score the order. OpenAI names fraud partners and PSPs it works with, as examples.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-013" />
- **Merchant plugin** (paths B and C), an MCP server built on OpenAI's plugin docs rather than on ACP.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-215" />

## Step by step

**Discovery, for every path**

1. **Claim the business and upload a feed.** OpenAI announced Businesses in ChatGPT on 2026-11-10, and its guide sends businesses to Business Hub. A business must verify its identity before it can upload products, chooses the countries and currencies it sells in, and delivers full snapshots by manual upload, a hosted URL or the Secure File Transfer Protocol (SFTP).<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-015" /> The rows follow OpenAI's own flat format: nine required fields from `item_id` to `price`, with the price in major units such as `79.99 USD`, plus `target_countries` and `store_country`; the standard upload targets the US.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-016" /> ACP's own feed model is different: an agent-hosted Feed API whose `Product` needs `id` and `variants`.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-113 clm-openai-acp-085" /> OpenAI's draft feed schema, published for planning and feedback only, follows that `Product` and `Variant` model without naming ACP.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-275" /> A feed does not update a cart or confirm a purchase.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-015" /> Shopify stores need no feed, because Shopify says their products reach ChatGPT through Shopify Catalog.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-035" />
2. **Ask (no protocol).** The shopper asks ChatGPT, or their dot, for the shoes.
3. **Discover.** ChatGPT looks for options in the public web, private API integrations and merchant product feeds, and a business profile tells the shopper about the seller.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-036" /> On 2026-03-24 OpenAI named Target, Sephora, Nordstrom, Lowe's, Best Buy, The Home Depot and Wayfair as integrated for discovery.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-179" />

**Path A: the dot pays with ChatGPT Wallet on the merchant's site (limited rollout since 2026-11-13)**

4. **Visit the site, signed (Web Bot Auth).** The dot's cloud browser requests the product and checkout pages. Each signed request carries `Signature`, `Signature-Input` and `Signature-Agent: "https://chatgpt.com"`.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-008" /> OpenAI is also rolling out user signals in a `Forwarded` header, such as `ip-country`, `locale` and `tz`.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-006" /> The visit needs no plugin and no product feed.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-011" />
5. **Verify at the edge.** The CDN checks that `Signature-Agent` matches `"https://chatgpt.com"` exactly, fetches the key from `https://chatgpt.com/.well-known/http-message-signatures-directory` and verifies the signature under RFC 9421.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-008" /> On 2026-11-14 and again on 2026-11-21 that directory held one Ed25519 key with an expiry a week ahead, so a verifier should fetch keys from it rather than hard-code them.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-010" /> The [Web Bot Auth](/orbit/web-bot-auth/) page covers the Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF) working group behind the mechanism.
6. **Fill the checkout.** The dot works through the merchant's normal checkout pages. TechCrunch reported that, according to OpenAI, the merchant receives what it needs to fulfill the order, such as the shipping address and contact details, from the dot (reported).<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-029" />
7. **Approve (ChatGPT Wallet).** ChatGPT shows the items, the total and the selected card, and the shopper approves with a security check such as Face ID or a fingerprint scan. The approval creates a record of what the shopper authorized.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-002" /> OpenAI's help article calls the check biometric verification, such as Face ID or Touch ID, on a supported device.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-293" /> OpenAI requires human review before any consequential action.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-012" />
8. **Get the payment token.** OpenAI's post says that after the approval the payment network provides a token with its own card number, security code and expiration date, so neither the business nor the agent sees the actual card; the Wallet terms let OpenAI ask a PSP, card network or issuer for that credential.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-003" /> CNBC reported that the token is specific to the transaction (reported).<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-027" />
9. **Pay in the existing form.** The dot enters the token in the merchant's card fields; no Wallet-specific integration is needed.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-004" /> OpenAI's help article says the dot uses a virtual card number linked to the selected card, so the number at checkout may differ from the physical card.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-297" /> The merchant receives a network token, not the card number, and can link it to the underlying account through the Payment Account Reference (PAR). The shopper's consent covers this transaction only.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-005" />
10. **Authorize (card rails).** The merchant's PSP authorizes the token like any card payment, and the merchant makes the fraud decision.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-004" /> Banks and networks evaluate the transaction, and the shopper keeps the card's rewards and protections for eligible disputes.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-031" /> Path A ends here.

**Path B: a merchant plugin with a website handoff**

11. **Call the plugin's tools (OpenAI plugins).** ChatGPT calls the merchant's MCP server for product lookup, carts and, where supported, checkout. OpenAI recommends a headless plugin because iframe-based MCP user interface (UI) is not supported in dots, and lookup and carts should work without sign-in.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-019" /> The server can identify ChatGPT through an OpenAI-managed client certificate over mutual Transport Layer Security (mTLS) and signs the shopper in with OAuth 2.1, or with Sign in with ChatGPT, which OpenAI says is not purchase approval.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-023 clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-024" />
12. **Return the checkout or a handoff.** The checkout tool returns the totals and the next step, plus a `continue_url`, ideally a one-time authenticated link that reopens the same cart.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-020" /> A plugin with its own UI may instead offer payment methods already saved with the merchant.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-251" />
13. **Continue on the site.** The shopper opens the `continue_url` and pays on the merchant's checkout, the generally available plugin path, with approval limited to physical goods.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-183 clm-openai-acp-250" /> How plugins and ChatGPT Wallet work together is not yet defined.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-020" />

**Path C: the ChatGPT payment sheet (beta, select marketplace partners)**

14. **Open the sheet (plugin UI).** The widget calls `window.openai.requestCheckout`.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-185" /> The plugin guidelines call this Instant Checkout. Plugin widgets run inside an iframe, and OpenAI says iframe-based MCP UI is not supported in dots.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-021 clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-034" />
15. **Confirm.** The shopper picks a payment method in the sheet and buys.
16. **Level 1 merchants only: Delegate Payment (ACP).** A merchant with a Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) Level 1 certificate can receive raw card data by implementing the ACP Delegate Payment endpoint, including allowance constraints.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-187" /> The request requires `payment_method`, `allowance`, `risk_signals` and `metadata`.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-075" /> The allowance requires `reason`, `max_amount`, `currency`, `checkout_session_id`, `merchant_id` and `expires_at`, and the only reason is `one_time`.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-076 clm-openai-acp-077" />
17. **Level 1: vault token (ACP).** The response is HTTP 201 with an id starting `vt_`.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-079" />
18. **Tool call (plugin).** ChatGPT sends a token for the selected payment method to the merchant's MCP server in a `complete_checkout` tool call.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-185" /> No source read says this token is an allowance-scoped delegated token in the general case.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-188" />
19. **Charge.** OpenAI lists Adyen, Checkout.com, Fiserv, PayPal, Stripe and Worldpay as supporting payment sheet processing.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-186" />

## Where controls apply

- **Before authorization.** Wallet asks the shopper to review and pass a security check for every purchase, and OpenAI requires human review before any consequential action. Site owners cannot yet choose which actions are allowed.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-002 clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-012" /> Plugins may sell only physical goods, and OpenAI's plugin guidelines prohibit categories such as tobacco and nicotine, weapons and prescription medications; that plugin list does not name alcohol, while OpenAI's separate Commerce policies bar alcoholic beverages over 0.5% ABV from product listings in its shopping experiences.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-022" /> In paths A and B the merchant's own checkout keeps its fraud screening.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-004" />
- **At signing.** The payment token exists only after the shopper's approval, and each approval leaves a record of what was authorized.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-002 clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-003" /> On the Delegate Payment path the allowance caps the amount and binds the token to one merchant and one checkout session.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-146 clm-openai-acp-147" />
- **At the edge.** The CDN verifies the Web Bot Auth signature before it applies any exception, and Cloudflare offers a per-agent setting for `dots, OpenAI` under Personal Agents.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-009" /> A plugin server can check ChatGPT's mTLS client certificate or its published egress IP ranges.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-023" /> On the Delegate Payment path the merchant authenticates ChatGPT with a bearer token agreed out of band, and ACP has no agent registry or key directory.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-140" />
- **At settlement.** The merchant's PSP and risk providers score the order. OpenAI names Forter, Riskified, Sardine and Signifyd among its fraud partners, and Forter says it identifies ChatGPT agent traffic for each Wallet transaction.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-013 clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-014" /> The merchant must not reuse the token for another purchase.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-005" /> For a delegated vault token the PSP enforces the allowance, and ACP defines no error code for a token used outside it.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-239" />
- **After the fact.** The shopper keeps the card's protections for eligible disputes, and the business keeps control of returns and customer service.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-031" /> OpenAI's FAQ says customers are ultimately responsible for the actions they take; that is OpenAI's position, not a card network rule.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-012" /> ACP has no revocation endpoint.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-148" />

## What is observable from outside

Very little, with one public exception. ChatGPT's signing keys are published at `https://chatgpt.com/.well-known/http-message-signatures-directory`, so anyone can check a signature.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-010" /> CDN verdicts appear in the site owner's dashboard, for example as `dots, OpenAI` in Cloudflare.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-009" /> Feeds, tool calls, approvals and tokens travel point to point between OpenAI, the merchant and the card rails.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-015 clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-003" /> The Wallet post gives no transaction, volume or merchant figures; its only usage figure is ChatGPT's 1.2 billion weekly users.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-038" /> There is no public explorer, indexer or registry of ACP merchants, and no official ACP transaction volume has been published.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-160 clm-openai-acp-199" />

## What can go wrong

- **The agent is blocked.** Security settings can block the signed cloud browser by accident. OpenAI recommends adding ChatGPT's agent profile to the firewall allowlist, after verifying the signatures, while keeping other controls.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-009" />
- **A legitimate order is declined.** Risk systems tuned to detect bots can make the dot's purchase fail, and the `Forwarded` signals that should help are still rolling out, so what a site receives may vary.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-006" /> Signifyd says it works with OpenAI on exactly this.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-014" />
- **Signals that cannot be trusted, or that leak.** Six of the seven `Forwarded` parameters are unregistered, RFC 7239 warns that any node on the path can change the header, and OpenAI does not say whether its signatures cover it.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-007" /> The header can also hand a site the shopper's IP address, network, country, locale and time zone.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-006" />
- **Credential reuse.** A merchant that stores the Wallet token for a later purchase breaks the one-transaction consent; recurring billing needs its own consent.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-005" /> OpenAI's help article adds that once the shopper agrees to a recurring plan, the merchant may store the credential and charge it again under those terms.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-297" />
- **Approval without an order.** OpenAI's help article says a ready-to-check-out status means the shopper's approval is complete, not that the merchant placed the order, and Wallet may not complete checkout at every store.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-297" />
- **Plugin payments without Wallet.** OpenAI has not defined how plugins and Wallet interact, so plugin checkout relies on sign-in, guest entry or the `continue_url`.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-020" />
- **Delegate Payment errors (path C, Level 1).** The endpoint returns codes such as `invalid_card`, `duplicate_request` and `idempotency_conflict`.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-238" />
- **Who bears the loss.** The merchant makes the fraud decision and handles the order and support, while the shopper keeps the card's dispute protections.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-004 clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-031" /> The Wallet terms make the merchant the seller and merchant of record and say OpenAI holds no purchase funds and does not decide or fund refunds or chargebacks unless the law requires it or OpenAI separately promises otherwise.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-295" /> Under ACP the merchant is merchant of record and carries refunds and chargebacks, as OpenAI's ACP docs said before the rewrite.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-046" />
- **Costs.** OpenAI's Wallet post, help article and partner playbook state no Wallet fee, and CNBC reported that OpenAI is not taking a cut to start (reported).<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-028 clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-027" /> OpenAI's merchant page says purchases that start in ChatGPT carry no fees, and Shopify says selling in ChatGPT costs only standard payment processing.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-259 clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-035" /> ACP itself charges no protocol fee; merchants pay their own PSP.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-196" />

## Variants

- **Delegated purchases, announced without a date.** OpenAI expects users to delegate purchases to their agent within limits such as spending caps, categories or time periods, and to pre-approve them with biometric checks and records of authorization.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-030" /> That would make the dot's purchases human-not-present; none of it is live on 2026-11-21, when OpenAI's help article still requires a biometric approval for each purchase.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-293" /> The Wallet terms (effective 2026-11-12) already provide for granting ChatGPT the authority to approve each purchase in advance.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-294" />
- **Other surfaces.** OpenAI's help article (updated 2026-11-19) says Wallet works only in conversations with a dot, not in Chat, Work or standalone Codex threads, while OpenAI's RSS summary of the Wallet post and the post's own page description name agents in ChatGPT Work, Codex and dots.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-026" /> OpenAI's Enterprise guide says a Use Wallet capability, off by default, will come to Enterprise workspaces, without a date.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-293" />
- **History: Instant Checkout in its 2025 form.** OpenAI announced Instant Checkout with ACP on 2025-09-29.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-007" /> At launch it supported single-item purchases from US Etsy sellers.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-167 clm-openai-acp-168" /> Stripe introduced the Shared Payment Token the same day.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-008" /> That flagship form was scaled back in March 2026, and trade press reported an OpenAI spokesperson saying Instant Checkout was moving to apps (reported).<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-224 clm-openai-acp-175" /> A January 2026 report said Shopify merchants would pay OpenAI 4% on those checkout sales (reported).<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-194" /> OpenAI's plugin guidelines still use the name for the marketplace payment sheet in path C.<Cite ids="clm-scn-chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout-021" />
- **A full ACP checkout session.** An agent can also run ACP's own checkout: `POST /checkout_sessions` creates a session and complete must return `completed` with an order.<Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-092 clm-openai-acp-095" /> That is the protocol as specified; this scenario follows what ChatGPT documents on 2026-11-21.
