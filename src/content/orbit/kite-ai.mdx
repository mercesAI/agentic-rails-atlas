Kite launched the Kite Chain mainnet and Kite Agent Passport on 2026-04-30; the passport gives an agent a programmable wallet with user-set spending limits and allowed destinations, and Kite says it integrates over 90 service providers (company figure). <Cite ids="src-orbit-kite-launch" /> Kite AI was listed as a general member at the x402 Foundation's operational launch on 2026-07-14. <Cite ids="src-x402-lf-july" /> The two products have since separated: Kite's docs changelog of 2026-11-17 says Kite Chain is no longer a Passport chain, so Passport refuses deposits, payments and transfers on it and no longer shows it in balances or deposit addresses. <Cite ids="src-orbit-kite-ai-changelog-20261117" />

As read 2026-11-21, Kite's service provider guide says a service that sells to Passport agents settles on Base, Polygon, Avalanche, Tempo or Arc in USDC, on Solana in USDC or PYUSD, or on Robinhood Chain in USDG, and accepts [x402](/p/x402/) or [MPP](/p/stripe-mpp/). <Cite ids="src-orbit-kite-ai-seller-20261121" /> For x402, Passport pays with the standard `exact` scheme through any x402-compatible facilitator that settles on the advertised network. The sample 402 response still uses the V1 wire format (`x402Version` 1, `maxAmountRequired` and the `X-PAYMENT` header), and in the EVM example the facilitator calls `transferWithAuthorization`. <Cite ids="src-orbit-kite-ai-seller-20261121 src-x402-repo" /> When the Atlas read the guide on 2026-10-19, its sample used a Kite scheme named `gokite-aa`, which the x402 specification does not define, and it recommended the Pieverse facilitator; Kite does not date the change. <Cite ids="src-orbit-kite-docs-seller src-orbit-kite-ai-seller-20261121" />

Google's [AP2](/p/ap2/) repository added Kite AI to its partners list on 2026-02-09 in pull request 161, whose description says Kite's team contributes to the AP2 specification and the a2a-x402 extension; v0.2 later removed the partners page. <Cite ids="src-ap2-partners-final src-ap2-gh-partners-history" /> Kite's home page lists Google Cloud (AP2) among its ecosystem partners, but no Kite product or documentation that implements AP2 was found on 2026-10-21. <Cite ids="src-orbit-kite-home src-orbit-kite-docs-seller" />
