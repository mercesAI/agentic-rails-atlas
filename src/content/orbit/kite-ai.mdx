Kite launched the Kite Chain mainnet and Kite Agent Passport on 2026-04-30; the passport gives an agent a programmable wallet with user-set spending limits and allowed destinations, and Kite says it integrates over 90 service providers (company figure). <Cite ids="src-orbit-kite-launch" /> Kite AI was listed as a general member at the x402 Foundation's operational launch on 2026-07-14. <Cite ids="src-x402-lf-july" /> Kite's developer docs describe how a service accepts [x402](/p/x402/) or MPP payments from Agent Passport agents (as of 2026-10-19). <Cite ids="src-orbit-kite-docs-seller" /> Their x402 sample uses the V1 wire format with an `X-PAYMENT` header and a Kite scheme named `gokite-aa` that the x402 specification does not define; the docs recommend the Pieverse facilitator. <Cite ids="src-orbit-kite-docs-seller src-x402-repo" /> Google's [AP2](/p/ap2/) repository added Kite AI to its partners list on 2026-02-09 in pull request 161, whose description says Kite's team contributes to the AP2 specification and the a2a-x402 extension; v0.2 later removed the partners page. <Cite ids="src-ap2-partners-final src-ap2-gh-partners-history" /> Kite's home page lists Google Cloud (AP2) among its ecosystem partners, but no Kite product or documentation that implements AP2 was found on 2026-10-21. <Cite ids="src-orbit-kite-home src-orbit-kite-docs-seller" />
