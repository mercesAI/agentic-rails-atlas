Amazon sued Perplexity in November 2025 (district docket opened 2025-11-04) over the Comet browser agent logging in to and shopping on Amazon.com for users. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amazon-perplexity-dockets" /> The district court granted a preliminary injunction, reported as dated 2026-03-09, that barred Comet's agent from password-protected areas of Amazon. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amazon-perplexity-geekwire src-orbit-amazon-perplexity-decrypt" /> In an opinion filed on 2026-08-04 in No. 26-1444 (which Perplexity's motion to dismiss cites as 184 F.4th 1083), the Ninth Circuit vacated the injunction and remanded, holding that on these facts the user rather than Perplexity accessed Amazon, using the agent as a tool, and describing its holding as narrow. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amazon-perplexity-ca9 src-orbit-amazon-perplexity-mtd" /> The court denied Amazon's petition for rehearing en banc on 2026-09-10, and its mandate issued on 2026-09-18. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amazon-perplexity-dockets" /> On 2026-09-21 Amazon filed an amended complaint that keeps the computer-access counts and adds a claim of tortious interference with contract, based on the Agent Terms it added to its Conditions of Use on 2025-05-30; it alleges that Comet presents its traffic as Google Chrome. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amazon-perplexity-fac" /> Those terms require an agent to identify itself, including by putting `Agent/[agent name]` in its user agent string, and not to mimic human browsing or get around CAPTCHAs: a self-declared label, not a signed identity such as [Web Bot Auth](/orbit/web-bot-auth/), [Visa TAP](/p/visa-tap/) or [Mastercard Agent Pay](/p/mastercard-agent-pay/) signatures. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amazon-conditions-of-use" /> Amazon also runs a shopping agent of its own on other merchants' sites. Buy for Me, which Amazon began testing in beta on 2025-04-03 with a subset of US customers, buys items Amazon does not sell on the brand's own website by passing the customer's encrypted name, address and payment details, while delivery, returns and customer service stay with the brand; Amazon says brands choose whether to take part. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amazon-buy-for-me" /> Alexa for Shopping, which Amazon launched on 2026-05-13 by combining Rufus and Alexa+, offers Buy for Me for eligible products from other stores, completing the whole purchase with the customer's primary address and credit card (as read 2026-11-21). Neither Amazon page names an agent protocol or a signature scheme. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amazon-buy-for-me src-orbit-amazon-alexa-for-shopping" /> Perplexity moved to dismiss the amended complaint on 2026-10-13, with a hearing set for 2026-11-20; as of 2026-11-14 there was no ruling and no petition for certiorari. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amazon-perplexity-mtd src-orbit-amazon-perplexity-dockets" /> Commentators note that site operators are left with terms of service and technical measures, which is the gap that signed agent identification such as TAP and Agent Pay targets; no source links the case to those programs. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amazon-perplexity-jonesday" />
