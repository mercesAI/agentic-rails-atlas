Amazon sued Perplexity in November 2025 (district docket opened 2025-11-04) over the Comet browser agent logging in to and shopping on Amazon.com for users. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amazon-perplexity-dockets" /> The district court granted a preliminary injunction, reported as dated 2026-03-09, that barred Comet's agent from password-protected areas of Amazon. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amazon-perplexity-geekwire src-orbit-amazon-perplexity-decrypt" /> In an opinion filed on 2026-08-04 in No. 26-1444, the Ninth Circuit vacated the injunction and remanded, holding that on these facts the user rather than Perplexity accessed Amazon, using the agent as a tool, and describing its holding as narrow. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amazon-perplexity-ca9" /> Commentators note that site operators are left with terms of service and technical measures, which is the gap that signed agent identification such as [Visa TAP](/p/visa-tap/) and [Mastercard Agent Pay](/p/mastercard-agent-pay/) targets; no source links the case to those programs. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amazon-perplexity-jonesday" />
