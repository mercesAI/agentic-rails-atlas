## What they are

ERC-7715, "Request Permissions from Wallets", is a wallet JSON-RPC standard. An app calls `wallet_requestExecutionPermissions` with a `chainId`, the session account `to` that will act, a `permission` with a `type` and `data`, and optional `rules` such as an `expiry`. The wallet answers with a `context` (the ERC-7710 permission context), any account `dependencies` and the `delegationManager` to call. Companion methods revoke a permission and list supported or granted ones. Permission types are open-ended; the text gives native token, ERC-20 and ERC-721 allowances as examples. <Cite ids="src-erc-7715" />

ERC-7710, "Smart Contract Delegation", is the on-chain half. A delegate calls `redeemDelegations(bytes[] _permissionContexts, bytes32[] _modes, bytes[] _executionCallData)` on a Delegation Manager, which checks the delegation and has the delegator's account execute the call, with modes and calldata in ERC-7579 format. How a delegation is obtained is out of scope, and ERC-7715 is one route. <Cite ids="src-erc-7710" /> Read together, ERC-7715 is the request a user approves in the wallet, and ERC-7710 is the receipt the agent later redeems.

## Status

Both are Draft. ERC-7715 was created on 2024-05-24, entered the ERCs repository on 2025-02-18 under its earlier title "Grant Permissions from Wallets", and was last simplified on 2026-01-16. <Cite ids="src-erc-7715 src-orbit-wallet-erc-7715-gitlog src-orbit-wallet-erc-7715-magicians" /> ERC-7710 grew out of a 2023-11-19 forum proposal, was created on 2024-05-20, entered the repository on 2024-12-12 and was last revised on 2025-06-10. <Cite ids="src-erc-7710 src-orbit-wallet-erc-7710-gitlog src-orbit-wallet-erc-7710-magicians" />

MetaMask Advanced Permissions implement ERC-7715 and redeem through ERC-7710. They need MetaMask v13.23.0 or later and a MetaMask smart account, and they offer allowance, periodic and stream permissions for ERC-20 and native tokens. <Cite ids="src-orbit-wallet-mm-advanced-permissions src-orbit-wallet-mm-supported-permissions" /> ERC-7710 names the MetaMask Delegation Framework as its complete reference implementation, and MetaMask renamed its Delegation Toolkit to the Smart Accounts Kit (docs change merged 2025-11-11). <Cite ids="src-erc-7710 src-orbit-wallet-mm-delegation-framework src-orbit-wallet-mm-sak-rebrand-pr src-orbit-wallet-mm-sak-repo" /> No other production wallet implementing ERC-7715 was confirmed.

## How they relate to the eight

[x402](/p/x402/) carries ERC-7710 directly: its `exact` EVM scheme has a third transfer method, `erc7710`, whose payload holds `delegationManager`, `permissionContext` and `delegator`, and the facilitator checks it by simulating `redeemDelegations`. The scheme lists ERC-7715 as one way to get such a delegation. <Cite ids="src-x402-scheme-exact-evm" /> MetaMask documents recurring x402 payments that pair an ERC-20 periodic permission with x402 clients. <Cite ids="src-orbit-wallet-mm-x402-recurring" /> This is the on-chain counterpart of a spending mandate: the user's limit lives in a delegation the wallet enforces, not in a signed document a merchant reads. ERC-8004 references neither standard, and no link to the other six was found. <Cite ids="src-erc-8004-spec" />
