ANP targets agents that find and talk to each other on the open internet rather than inside one platform. A 2025 survey of agent interoperability protocols groups it with MCP, IBM's Agent Communication Protocol and A2A, and describes it as using decentralized identifiers (DIDs) and JSON-LD for open-network discovery and secure collaboration. <Cite ids="src-arxiv-2505-02279" /> None of the eight protocols reference ANP in their specifications.
