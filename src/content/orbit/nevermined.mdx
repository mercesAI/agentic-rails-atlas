Nevermined runs an [x402](/p/x402/) facilitator and describes an x402 Smart Accounts Extension built on ERC-4337 smart accounts for plans, credits and subscriptions. <Cite ids="src-orbit-nevermined-facilitator" /> Its docs show an ADK demo that carries Nevermined x402 payments inside [AP2](/p/ap2/) and A2A messages. <Cite ids="src-orbit-nevermined-ap2" /> The facilitator also accepts MPP, which Nevermined's docs expand as "Merchant Payment Protocol", a different name from the Machine Payments Protocol. <Cite ids="src-orbit-nevermined-facilitator" /> The company has also published a third-party review of Visa Intelligent Commerce, and its SDK `nevermined-io/payments` is Apache-2.0. <Cite ids="src-nevermined-vic src-orbit-nevermined-repo" />
