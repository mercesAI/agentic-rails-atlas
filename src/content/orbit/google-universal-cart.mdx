Google announced Universal Cart on 2026-05-19 as a shopping product, distinct from the [UCP](/p/ucp/) protocol, that checks out through UCP with Google Pay while the brand remains merchant of record. <Cite ids="src-ucp-google-universal-cart" /> US rollout across Search and the Gemini app was scheduled for summer 2026, with YouTube and Gmail later. <Cite ids="src-ucp-google-universal-cart" /> Universal Cart launched in the US on 2026-10-15 across Google Search and the Gemini app, with checkout through UCP: Google Pay in the cart, or a transfer of the items to the merchant's cart. Google named Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta Beauty, Walmart, Wayfair and many Shopify merchants; merchants remain seller of record, and the post does not say which of them have native checkout. <Cite ids="src-ucp-google-universal-cart-launch" /> Google's 2026-05-19 post said [AP2](/p/ap2/) would come to Google products "in the coming months", starting with Gemini Spark, and announced UCP checkout expansion to Canada and Australia, then the UK. <Cite ids="src-ucp-google-universal-cart" /> The 2026-10-15 launch post does not mention AP2. <Cite ids="src-ucp-google-universal-cart-launch" /> UCP direct checkout, not Universal Cart, began rolling out to Canadian shoppers on 2026-11-04 on AI Mode and the Gemini app; Google's Canada post does not mention Universal Cart, which its own posts still describe as rolling out in the US only. <Cite ids="src-ucp-google-canada src-ucp-google-universal-cart-launch" /> On 2026-11-17 Google said Gemini now uses AP2 for purchases made with its new Google Pay checkout in Tasks in Gemini, the product its help center says was formerly called Spark; that post does not mention Universal Cart. <Cite ids="src-ap2-google-gemini-tasks-20261117 src-ap2-gemini-tasks-help" />
