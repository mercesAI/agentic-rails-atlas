Google announced Universal Cart on 2026-05-19 as a shopping product, distinct from the [UCP](/p/ucp/) protocol, that checks out through UCP with Google Pay while the brand remains merchant of record. <Cite ids="src-ucp-google-universal-cart" /> US rollout across Search and the Gemini app was scheduled for summer 2026, with YouTube and Gmail later; whether the rollout completed was not verified. <Cite ids="src-ucp-google-universal-cart" /> The same post says [AP2](/p/ap2/) will come to Google products "in the coming months", starting with Gemini Spark, and announces UCP checkout expansion to Canada and Australia, then the UK. <Cite ids="src-ucp-google-universal-cart" />
