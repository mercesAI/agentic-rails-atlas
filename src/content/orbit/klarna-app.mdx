Klarna launched the Agentic Product Protocol on 2025-12-15 as an open product-feed specification and API, saying both were available that day. <Cite ids="src-orbit-klarna-app" /> Klarna claims more than 100 million products and 400 million prices across 12 markets (company figures, as of 2025-12-15). <Cite ids="src-orbit-klarna-app" /> As a feed format it overlaps with [ACP](/p/openai-acp/) product feeds and [UCP](/p/ucp/) catalog capabilities, and Klarna is also a listed UCP endorser. <Cite ids="src-ucp-site" />
