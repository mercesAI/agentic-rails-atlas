goose is an open-source AI agent from Block that gains its abilities through MCP servers. Block contributed it to the Agentic AI Foundation when the Linux Foundation formed the foundation on 2025-12-09, together with Anthropic's MCP and OpenAI's AGENTS.md. <Cite ids="src-gloss-aaif-lf" /> None of the eight protocols reference goose. As an MCP client it could in principle call the MCP transports that [x402](/p/x402/) and [MPP](/p/stripe-mpp/) define, but that was not verified. <Cite ids="src-x402-transport-mcp-v2 src-stripe-mpp-mcp-transport" />
