Crossmint launched the Agentic Cards API publicly on 2026-06-02, built on Visa Intelligent Commerce and Basis Theory: developers connect eligible US-issued Visa cards to AI agents as tokenized credentials with spend limits, and issuer approval and controls still apply. <Cite ids="src-orbit-crossmint-launch" /> The SDK `Crossmint/crossmint-sdk` is Apache-2.0. <Cite ids="src-orbit-crossmint-sdk" /> The launch post does not say whether [Visa TAP](/p/visa-tap/) is used, so the link to TAP runs through Visa Intelligent Commerce tokens.
