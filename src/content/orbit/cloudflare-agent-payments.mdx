Cloudflare co-founded the x402 Foundation with Coinbase (announced 2025-09-23), and its Monetization Gateway entered closed beta on 2026-09-30 for eligible US-based sellers and buyers, settling USDC on Base through Coinbase's [x402](/p/x402/) facilitator; the separate credit-backed `cloudflare:402` batch-settlement network in the x402 spec is not the beta's settlement path. <Cite ids="src-x402-cf-blog-foundation src-x402-cf-mg-beta src-x402-cf-changelog src-x402-scheme-batch-cf src-x402-gh-pr-1145" /> On 2026-11-14 Cloudflare's docs still described the gateway as a closed beta for buyers and sellers based in the United States (page last updated 2026-11-01). <Cite ids="src-orbit-cloudflare-mg-docs" /> Fixed prices use the x402 `exact` scheme. Variable prices use `upto`: the seller's origin reports the actual amount, capped at the buyer's authorized maximum, in a `PAYMENT-SETTLEMENT` header that the gateway removes before the response reaches the buyer (documented on a page last updated 2026-09-30). <Cite ids="src-orbit-cloudflare-mg-payment-validation" /> Cloudflare Wallets were announced on 2026-08-04, and on 2026-11-14 handle reservation was still the only part open; a reserved handle cannot yet send, receive or hold funds. <Cite ids="src-orbit-cloudflare-wallets-blog src-orbit-cloudflare-wallets-pr src-orbit-cloudflare-wallets-docs" /> NET Dollar, announced on 2025-09-25 as a planned US dollar-backed stablecoin, had no launch found as of 2026-11-14: neither the press releases listed on Cloudflare's press page that day (the newest dated 2026-11-05) nor its third-quarter 2026 results (2026-11-05) mention it. <Cite ids="src-orbit-cloudflare-net-dollar src-orbit-cloudflare-q3-2026" /> Cloudflare verifies [Visa TAP](/p/visa-tap/) and [Mastercard Agent Pay](/p/mastercard-agent-pay/) signatures at its edge and, since 2026-07-01, lists signed agents as a category of Verified Bots; its per-agent access policies for personal agents (2026-11-04) are covered on the [Cloudflare Verified Bots](/orbit/cloudflare-verified-bots/) page. <Cite ids="src-visa-tap-cf-blog src-cf-verified-bots src-orbit-cf-personal-agents-changelog" /> The Radar API needs an API token, and Radar data is licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0. <Cite ids="src-orbit-cloudflare-radar-api" />
