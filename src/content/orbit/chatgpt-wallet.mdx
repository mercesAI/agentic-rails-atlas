## What it is

ChatGPT Wallet is OpenAI's way for an agent in ChatGPT to pay on a user's behalf. OpenAI introduced it on 2026-11-13 and says it lets agents pay without access to the user's actual card details while requiring a security check for every purchase. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet" /> As read 2026-11-21, the post says Wallet was built in partnership with American Express, Chase, Mastercard and Visa; its launch-day text and launch-day coverage named American Express, Mastercard and Visa only, and OpenAI did not mark the change. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-orbit-chatgpt-wallet-neowin" /> It launched in dots, the always-on agents OpenAI introduced on 2026-09-29, which live in the cloud with their own computer and browser. <Cite ids="src-scn-chatgpt-dots-docs src-openai-news-rss-20261114" /> Wallet is not a new checkout protocol: the dot uses the merchant's existing website checkout, and OpenAI's merchant guide says no Wallet-specific integration is required. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-openai-commerce-accept-payments" />

## How a purchase works

1. The user adds a card once. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet" />
2. The dot finds the item and works through the merchant's own checkout with its cloud browser. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-openai-commerce-browser-access" /> TechCrunch reported that, according to OpenAI, the merchant receives what it needs to fulfill the order, such as the shipping address and contact details, from the dot (reported). <Cite ids="src-scn-chatgpt-techcrunch-wallet" />
3. Before paying, ChatGPT shows the items, the total and the selected card, and the user approves with a security check such as Face ID or a fingerprint scan. Each approval creates a record of the purchase the user authorized. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet" />
4. OpenAI's post says the payment network then provides a payment token, a stand-in for the card with its own card number, security code and expiration date, and that the card is charged without Wallet sharing the actual card details with the business or the agent. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet" /> OpenAI's help article says the dot then uses a virtual card number linked to the selected card, so the number at checkout may differ from the physical card, and that Wallet generates a payment token for each use; CNBC also reported that the token is specific to the transaction (reported). <Cite ids="src-openai-wallet-help-purchases src-scn-chatgpt-cnbc-wallet" />
5. The merchant's checkout processes the payment like any card payment, and the business handles the order, fulfillment, customer support and fraud decisions. <Cite ids="src-openai-commerce-accept-payments" /> When ChatGPT reports that a purchase is ready to check out, the approval is complete, but that does not confirm that the merchant placed the order, and Wallet may not complete checkout at every store. <Cite ids="src-openai-wallet-help-purchases" />

Users keep their card's rewards and protections for eligible disputes, banks and networks evaluate the transactions, and businesses keep control of pricing, fulfillment, returns and customer service. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet" /> For help, OpenAI's article sends users to the merchant for order status, cancellations, returns and refunds, to the card issuer for declined, disputed or unrecognized charges, and to OpenAI Support for Wallet errors or a purchase that differs from what they approved. <Cite ids="src-openai-wallet-help-purchases" /> OpenAI says it expects, over time, to let users delegate purchases to their agent within spending limits, categories or time periods and pre-approve them with biometric checks and records of authorization. It gives no date, and on 2026-11-21 every purchase still needs the user's biometric approval. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-openai-wallet-help-purchases" /> The scenario [From ChatGPT discovery to merchant checkout](/scenarios/chatgpt-discovery-to-checkout/) walks through the whole sequence, next to the plugin and payment sheet paths.

## Terms and data

OpenAI's ChatGPT Wallet terms took effect on 2026-11-12. They make the merchant the seller and merchant of record and say OpenAI is not a bank, card issuer, payment network, lender or merchant for Wallet purchases and holds no purchase funds. <Cite ids="src-orbit-chatgpt-wallet-terms" /> OpenAI does not decide or fund merchant refunds or card chargebacks unless the law requires it or OpenAI separately promises otherwise, the cardholder agreement and issuer and network rules, including dispute rights, still apply, and OpenAI may set transaction, merchant, category, card, account, device or geographic limits. <Cite ids="src-orbit-chatgpt-wallet-terms" /> After an approval, OpenAI may ask a payment service provider, card network or issuer to create or provide a tokenized, virtual or other credential linked to the card. <Cite ids="src-orbit-chatgpt-wallet-terms" /> Under the Wallet Privacy Notice of the same date, a service provider collects the card number and security code, OpenAI receives a tokenized credential with limited card details, and OpenAI says it does not sell Wallet data or share it for targeted advertising. <Cite ids="src-orbit-chatgpt-wallet-privacy" />

## What merchants need

OpenAI published its Commerce in ChatGPT partner playbook alongside Wallet; it explains what a merchant can check, and browser access needs neither a plugin nor a product feed. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-openai-commerce-playbook src-openai-commerce-agent-access" />

- **Let the cloud browser in.** ChatGPT signs certain cloud browser requests with Web Bot Auth (OpenAI's guidance covers these requests, not its web crawlers): a `Signature` header, a `Signature-Input` header and `Signature-Agent: "https://chatgpt.com"`, verified under RFC 9421 with keys from `https://chatgpt.com/.well-known/http-message-signatures-directory`. <Cite ids="src-openai-commerce-agent-access src-rfc9421" /> Akamai, Cloudflare, HUMAN and Vercel verify these signatures automatically; the guide names the traffic ChatGPT Agent at Akamai and HUMAN, `dots, OpenAI` under Personal Agents or bot tag `chatgpt-agent` with detection ID `129220581` at Cloudflare, and `chatgpt-operator` at Vercel. <Cite ids="src-openai-commerce-agent-access" /> As read 2026-11-21, the guide recommends adding ChatGPT's agent profile to the firewall's allowlist, while keeping other security controls in place, so that visits on a user's behalf go through reliably, and verifying the signatures before applying an allowlist exception for signed requests. <Cite ids="src-openai-commerce-agent-access" /> That is advice, not a requirement: OpenAI's companion page says to change settings only if security settings block verified visits, and a Vercel-hosted site whose rules do not block the traffic needs no extra configuration. <Cite ids="src-openai-commerce-browser-access src-openai-commerce-agent-access" /> On 2026-11-14 and again on 2026-11-21 the key directory held one Ed25519 key with an expiry a week ahead, so a verifier should fetch keys from it rather than hard-code them. <Cite ids="src-scn-chatgpt-key-directory" /> The [Web Bot Auth](/orbit/web-bot-auth/) page covers the Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF) working group behind the mechanism.
- **Accept the token in the existing checkout.** The merchant receives a network token instead of the card number and can use the Payment Account Reference (PAR) to link it to the underlying account. <Cite ids="src-openai-commerce-accept-payments" /> The customer consents to that one transaction, so the merchant must not reuse or save the credential for another purchase. Subscriptions need the customer's consent for recurring charges and the processor's merchant-initiated transaction practice, and Wallet sits beside any existing card-on-file flow. <Cite ids="src-openai-commerce-accept-payments" /> OpenAI's help article adds that if the user agrees to a recurring plan, the merchant may store the credential and charge it again under those terms. <Cite ids="src-openai-wallet-help-purchases" />
- **Tune risk systems.** Risk systems built to catch bots can flag legitimate agent purchases and decline or cancel them. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-openai-commerce-accept-payments" /> OpenAI is rolling out user signals in the `Forwarded` header: `asn`, `for` (the IP address observed for the user), `ip-country`, `ip-prefix`, `locale`, `tz` and `user-agent`. <Cite ids="src-openai-commerce-accept-payments" /> Of these seven, only `for` is in the Internet Assigned Numbers Authority (IANA) registry of Forwarded parameters, RFC 7239 warns that any node on the path can change the header, and OpenAI does not say whether its signatures cover it. <Cite ids="src-scn-chatgpt-iana-forwarded src-scn-chatgpt-rfc7239 src-openai-commerce-agent-access" /> The header also passes the shopper's IP address, network, country, locale and time zone to the sites that receive it. <Cite ids="src-openai-commerce-accept-payments" />

OpenAI's agent access FAQ adds that site owners cannot yet control which actions users take, that OpenAI requires human review before any consequential action, and that customers are ultimately responsible for the actions they take. The last point is OpenAI's position, not a card network rule. <Cite ids="src-openai-commerce-agent-access" /> For plugins, OpenAI says it is still working on how third-party plugins and Wallet interact. <Cite ids="src-openai-commerce-build-plugin" />

## Partners and availability

- **Card networks and a card issuer.** As read 2026-11-21, OpenAI's post names American Express, Chase, Mastercard and Visa as Wallet's partners. Its launch-day text, as captured at 2026-11-13T16:04:07Z, and launch-day coverage named American Express, Mastercard and Visa, and OpenAI did not mark the change. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-orbit-chatgpt-wallet-neowin" /> Chase takes part as a card issuer: OpenAI's help article lists eligible Chase credit cards, accepts them only from the primary cardholder, not an authorized user, and has the user approve the connection in the Chase mobile app, by QR code when starting on a computer. <Cite ids="src-openai-wallet-help-purchases" /> The post's quotes come from American Express, Mastercard and Visa executives, and Chase appears only in the partner sentence. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet" /> OpenAI's post quotes American Express's Luke Gebb calling Wallet the first American Express Agentic Commerce Experiences (ACE) integration, but American Express itself had published nothing on Wallet as of 2026-11-21, so that status rests on OpenAI's post (reported). <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-scn-chatgpt-amex-newsroom" /> Apart from that quote the post names no card network program. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet" /> Mastercard's CEO said on 2026-10-29 that a broad partnership with OpenAI includes the use of [Mastercard Agent Pay](/p/mastercard-agent-pay/) (reported), and Visa announced an OpenAI collaboration under [Visa Intelligent Commerce](/orbit/visa-intelligent-commerce/) on 2026-06-10, but no source says which program issues Wallet tokens. <Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-281 clm-openai-acp-282" />
- **Fraud and payment partners.** OpenAI names Forter, Riskified, Sardine and Signifyd among its fraud prevention partners and Adyen, Checkout.com and Stripe among its payment service providers, as examples rather than complete lists. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet" /> Forter says it identifies ChatGPT agent traffic for each Wallet transaction and feeds that into its fraud decisions, and Signifyd says it works with OpenAI so that legitimate agent orders are not declined. <Cite ids="src-scn-chatgpt-forter-wallet src-scn-chatgpt-signifyd-wallet" />
- **Who can use it.** Wallet began rolling out on 2026-11-13 in dots, initially for ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US aged 18 and older with eligible US-issued cards. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet" /> OpenAI's help article (updated 2026-11-19) says it works in conversations with a dot, which the user sets up first: "Wallet isn't available in Chat, Work, or standalone Codex threads." Approving a purchase requires biometric verification, such as Face ID or Touch ID, on a supported device. <Cite ids="src-openai-wallet-help-purchases" /> Eligible cards are US-issued American Express, Chase, Mastercard and Visa cards, and the user must live in the US and have a US billing address for the card. The bank may ask to verify the card during setup, some US-issued debit cards work but Chase debit cards do not, and virtual cards cannot be added at launch. <Cite ids="src-openai-wallet-help-purchases" /> The dots-only limit was already reported on launch day (reported), while the post's own page description and OpenAI's RSS summary still name agents in ChatGPT Work, Codex and dots (as read 2026-11-21). <Cite ids="src-orbit-chatgpt-wallet-neowin src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-openai-acp-news-rss-20261121" /> OpenAI's Enterprise guide says Wallet is off by default for Enterprise workspaces and that admins will be able to grant a Use Wallet capability once the setting is available; it gives no date. <Cite ids="src-orbit-chatgpt-wallet-dots-admin" />
- **Fees.** OpenAI's post, help article and partner playbook state no fee for Wallet purchases, and CNBC reported that OpenAI is not taking a cut of transactions to start (reported). <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-openai-wallet-help-purchases src-openai-commerce-playbook src-scn-chatgpt-cnbc-wallet" />

## What it is not

- **Not the Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP).** OpenAI's Wallet post and its partner playbook do not name ACP, and the post never mentions Instant Checkout, Delegate Payment or Shared Payment Tokens. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-openai-commerce-playbook" /> OpenAI's post says the payment network provides the token, and the Wallet terms let OpenAI ask a payment service provider, card network or issuer for the credential; none of these sources names an ACP Delegate Payment vault or a Stripe Shared Payment Token. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-orbit-chatgpt-wallet-terms" /> OpenAI's merchant page still says ChatGPT shopping is built on ACP, which supports product discovery today. <Cite ids="src-chatgpt-merchants" />
- **Not Instant Checkout.** OpenAI's plugin guidelines use that name for the ChatGPT payment sheet, a beta for select marketplace partners that a plugin opens from its own interface. <Cite ids="src-scn-chatgpt-plugin-guidelines src-openai-apps-monetization src-scn-chatgpt-plugin-ui" />
- **Not Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol (TAP) or Mastercard's agent signature.** ChatGPT identifies itself with plain Web Bot Auth and the `Signature-Agent` value `"https://chatgpt.com"`, and OpenAI's docs do not mention TAP's `agent-browser-auth` and `agent-payer-auth` tags or Mastercard's `Agent-pay-auth` tag. <Cite ids="src-openai-commerce-agent-access src-visa-tap-spec src-mcap-dev-level1" />
- **Not a merchant integration project.** OpenAI says no Wallet-specific integration is required. <Cite ids="src-openai-commerce-accept-payments" />
- **Not autonomous yet.** OpenAI's help article and dots controls page require a biometric approval for each payment, and the post describes delegated purchases as a future expectation without a date. <Cite ids="src-openai-wallet-help-purchases src-orbit-chatgpt-wallet-dots-controls src-openai-chatgpt-wallet" /> The Wallet terms, effective 2026-11-12, already provide for a user granting ChatGPT the authority to approve each purchase in advance, while those product pages still require the biometric approval each time. <Cite ids="src-orbit-chatgpt-wallet-terms src-openai-wallet-help-purchases" />

## How it relates to the eight

[ACP](/p/openai-acp/) shares a steward with Wallet, since OpenAI and Stripe are its founding maintainers, but OpenAI documents no role for ACP in Wallet. <Cite ids="clm-openai-acp-012 src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-openai-commerce-playbook" /> Mastercard and Visa are among Wallet's named partners, yet no source ties it to [Mastercard Agent Pay](/p/mastercard-agent-pay/) or [Visa TAP](/p/visa-tap/), and ChatGPT's request signatures do not use either network's tags. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-openai-commerce-agent-access src-visa-tap-spec src-mcap-dev-level1" /> No relation to [x402](/p/x402/), the [Machine Payments Protocol](/p/stripe-mpp/), the [Universal Commerce Protocol](/p/ucp/), the [Agent Payments Protocol](/p/ap2/) or [ERC-8004](/p/erc-8004/) is documented in OpenAI's post or commerce docs. <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-openai-commerce-playbook" />
