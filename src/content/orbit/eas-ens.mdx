The Ethereum Attestation Service is an open contract system for attestations on EVM chains: anyone registers a schema and then attests against it, on-chain or off-chain, with optional resolver contracts. It has no EIP number. <Cite ids="src-orbit-eas-site src-orbit-eas-contracts-repo" /> Its mainnet contract was deployed in block 16756728 on 2023-03-04, and the project's site reports more than 9.5 million attestations and 450,000 attesters, its own figures as of 2026-10-11. <Cite ids="src-orbit-eas-mainnet-deployment src-orbit-eas-site" /> None of the eight protocols cites EAS.

ENS resolves human-readable names to addresses and records; its core proposal, now ENSIP-1, dates from 2016-04-04, and the ENS DAO governs the protocol. <Cite ids="src-orbit-ensip-1 src-orbit-ens-dao-docs" /> Two Draft ENSIPs target agents. ENSIP-25, merged 2026-02-18, defines a text record `agent-registration[<registry>][<agentId>]` with which a name owner confirms a link to an agent registry entry, using the [ERC-8004](/p/erc-8004/) Identity Registry as its example. ENSIP-26, merged 2026-05-06, adds `agent-context` and `agent-endpoint[<protocol>]` keys aligned with ERC-8004 service names. <Cite ids="src-orbit-ensip-index src-orbit-ensip-25 src-orbit-ensip-26" /> From the other side, ERC-8004's registration example lists an `ENS` service, and agents may advertise an ENS name. <Cite ids="src-erc-8004-spec" />
