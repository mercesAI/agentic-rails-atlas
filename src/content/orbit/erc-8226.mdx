ERC-8226 splits compliance into three layers: token compliance (for example ERC-7943 or ERC-3643), mandate compliance (this ERC) and agent identity (a registry such as [ERC-8004](/p/erc-8004/)). <Cite ids="src-erc-8226 src-ap2-erc8226" /> The principal signs a mandate with EIP-712 (EIP-1271 for contract accounts) that sets validity dates and per-transaction and cumulative caps; tokens call `canExecute` before acting and `recordExecution` to consume the cap, enforcers can freeze an agent or principal, and only the principal can revoke. <Cite ids="src-erc-8226" /> Created on 2026-04-12 and first merged into `ethereum/ERCs` on 2026-05-12, it moved to one mandate per agent, principal and asset on 2026-10-09; its Ethereum Magicians thread had 50 posts by then. <Cite ids="src-orbit-erc-8226-prs src-orbit-erc-8226-magicians" /> The spec text does not mention AP2, x402 or any card network; see [ERC-3643](/orbit/erc-3643/) for how it bridges token compliance and agent identity. <Cite ids="src-erc-8226 src-orbit-erc-8226-brickken" />
