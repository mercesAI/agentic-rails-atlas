The Agent Name Service started as `draft-narajala-ans-00` on 2025-05-16, with a matching arXiv paper (2505.10609) a day earlier. <Cite ids="src-gloss-ans-draft src-orbit-ans-arxiv-2505-10609" /> Its successor `draft-narajala-courtney-ansv2` had revisions `-00` and `-01` on 2026-04-13 as an Independent Submission aimed at Informational status. Revision `-02` (2026-10-26, same five authors) rewrites it as an architecture document for a domain-anchored identity layer and leaves the detailed rules to separate specifications numbered ANS-0 to ANS-6. ANS v2 still anchors an agent to a DNS domain: a Registration Authority checks domain ownership with ACME-style challenges, issues certificates that bind a versioned name of the form `ans://v{version}.{agentHost}`, and seals lifecycle events into an append-only transparency log. In `-02`, discovery uses the SVCB records of DNS for AI Discovery (DNS-AID) by default, with an older TXT format only as an opt-in profile, and an `_ans-badge` TXT record points to the agent's log entry. A verifier works on either a badge tier, where it queries the transparency log when it connects, or a SCITT (Supply Chain Integrity, Transparency and Trust) tier, where it checks a receipt and a status token locally; the Bronze, Silver and Gold tiers of `-01` are gone. It references MCP and A2A but none of the eight protocols (as of 2026-11-21). <Cite ids="src-orbit-ans-v2-draft" /> A separate draft from 2026-07-25 proposes an IANA registry for ANS protocol identifiers. <Cite ids="src-orbit-ans-protocol-registry-draft" /> Agent discovery now also has a working group at the Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF): Discovery of Agents With Names (DAWN), chartered on 2026-10-22, reviewed candidate drafts at IETF 127 on 2026-11-19, among them DNS-AID, now `draft-mozleywilliams-dawn-dnsaid-00`, next to proposals for DNS-based agent naming, multicast DNS discovery and registry-assisted resolution, and it had adopted none of them on 2026-11-21. <Cite ids="src-orbit-dawn-wg src-orbit-dawn-ietf127-agenda src-orbit-dawn-dnsaid-draft" />

NANDA (Networked AI Agents in Decentralized Architecture) is an MIT Media Lab project. <Cite ids="src-gloss-nanda-mit" /> Its paper on the NANDA Index and verified AgentFacts appeared on arXiv on 2025-07-18, followed by an enterprise paper on 2025-08-05. <Cite ids="src-orbit-nanda-arxiv-2507-14263 src-orbit-nanda-arxiv-2508-03101" /> AgentFacts is a JSON Schema for an agent's identity, provider, endpoints, capabilities and certification, and the NANDA Index v2 maps `urn:air:` identifiers to an AI catalog, an A2A Agent Card or another card, without hosting agents. <Cite ids="src-orbit-nanda-gh-projnanda src-orbit-nanda-agentfacts-format src-orbit-nanda-index-v2" /> The registry-assisted resolution candidate at DAWN, `draft-raskar-agentic-web-federated-resolution-03` (2026-11-16), has authors from the MIT Media Lab, ProjectNanda and MIT and presents NandaIndex as one reference implementation, with no global registry assumed. <Cite ids="src-orbit-nanda-federated-resolution-draft src-orbit-dawn-ietf127-agenda" /> The project's `nanda-payments` repository implements "x402-NP", which reuses the [x402](/p/x402/) HTTP 402 pattern with NANDA Points instead of crypto. <Cite ids="src-orbit-nanda-payments-x402np" />
