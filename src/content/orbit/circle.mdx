Nanopayments, live on mainnet since 2026-04-29, let a buyer sign EIP-3009 authorizations off-chain at zero gas for amounts from $0.000001 USDC, which Gateway batches and settles in bulk inside the [x402](/p/x402/) flow. <Cite ids="src-x402-circle-nanopayments src-x402-fee-circle" /> Circle says bulk settlement means neither party pays per-transaction fees. Moving funds out of Gateway costs a gas fee per burn that depends on the source chain, and crosschain transfers add a percentage transfer fee, which Circle waives when Arc is the source or the destination. Circle loads the current fees from its fee API; the $0.01 gas fee and 0.005 percent transfer fee on its fees page are inputs to a worked example, not a price list (as of 2026-11-14). <Cite ids="src-x402-fee-circle-gateway" />

Circle also runs a facilitator of its own. Facilitator Service, launched on 2026-09-16, verifies buyer-signed x402 payments for sellers and settles USDC on Arc, Base and Polygon PoS with the exact scheme, and since 2026-10-13 with upto. Circle broadcasts every transfer, pays the settlement gas and screens both parties; settling needs a Circle API key, and a keyless trial lets a seller start without an account. <Cite ids="src-x402-circle-facilitator-service src-x402-circle-release-notes" /> Circle says the service does not hold, control or transmit user funds, and that settlements on Base and Polygon PoS carry reorganization risk while Arc settlements are final. The page publishes no price (as of 2026-11-14). <Cite ids="src-x402-circle-facilitator-service" /> On the buyer side, Circle's Agent Marketplace (2026-07-31) is a curated catalog of x402 services that accept USDC. <Cite ids="src-x402-circle-release-notes" />

Circle says it launched the Agent Stack (policy-controlled Agent Wallets plus Nanopayments) in May 2026 and opened Arc's public mainnet on 2026-09-16 with a permissioned founding validator cohort that includes BlackRock, DTCC, Mastercard and Visa. <Cite ids="src-orbit-circle-arc-mainnet src-orbit-circle-arc-validators src-orbit-circle-arc-theblock" /> The Arc release says Arc has not been reviewed or approved by the New York State Department of Financial Services or any other regulator. <Cite ids="src-orbit-circle-arc-mainnet" /> It also claims that USDC accounts for 98.8% of agent-driven transaction volume, linking a public Dune query of x402 volume by token. The release gives no method or date range (as of 2026-09-16). <Cite ids="src-orbit-circle-arc-mainnet src-orbit-circle-dune-x402-token" />

Arc's contract address page lists the [ERC-8004](/p/erc-8004/) Identity, Reputation and Validation registries on Arc Mainnet and Arc Testnet, and a reference contract for ERC-8183 job escrow on Arc Testnet. That is a chain operator documenting addresses on its own chain: the ERC-8004 maintainers list no Validation Registry deployment, and their README did not list Arc Mainnet on 2026-11-14. <Cite ids="src-erc-8004-arc-contract-addresses src-erc-8004-contracts-pr-100" />

On the research side, Circle presented Anduin on 2026-11-13: a way to rate x402 service providers from the payment graph, who pays whom, so that looking trusted to an agent costs real payments at the services that agent already uses. Circle says that on 66 million x402 payments (66.1 million settlements worth $6.1 million over 157 days, 2026-03-23 to 2026-08-26, on 16 networks) Anduin recommends providers about as accurately as collaborative filtering, the best standard method, which it does not beat, while being about 23 times more expensive to manipulate in the tested attack. It needs no change to x402, HTTP payment headers or ERC standards, and Circle presents it as research, not released software. <Cite ids="src-orbit-circle-anduin" /> The paper is arXiv 2611.05586 by Ramji, Vander Vos, Zeng and Le; on 2026-11-21 its only version was v1 of 2026-11-03. <Cite ids="src-orbit-circle-anduin-arxiv" /> Circle also counts fewer than 4,000 voluntary on-chain ratings, such as ERC-8004 feedback, across those payments, and cites that figure as evidence that voluntary ratings are too sparse to rank providers. <Cite ids="src-orbit-circle-anduin" />
