Nanopayments, live on mainnet since 2026-04-29, let a buyer sign EIP-3009 authorizations off-chain at zero gas for amounts from $0.000001 USDC, which Gateway batches and settles in bulk inside the [x402](/p/x402/) flow. <Cite ids="src-x402-circle-nanopayments src-x402-fee-circle" /> Circle says bulk settlement means neither party pays per-transaction fees. Moving funds out of Gateway costs a gas fee per burn, for example $0.01 from Base, and crosschain transfers add 0.005 percent (as of 2026-10-19). <Cite ids="src-x402-fee-circle src-x402-fee-circle-gateway" /> Circle says it launched the Agent Stack (policy-controlled Agent Wallets plus Nanopayments) in May 2026 and opened Arc's public mainnet on 2026-09-16 with a permissioned founding validator cohort that includes BlackRock, DTCC, Mastercard and Visa. <Cite ids="src-orbit-circle-arc-mainnet src-orbit-circle-arc-validators src-orbit-circle-arc-theblock" /> The Arc release says Arc has not been reviewed or approved by the New York State Department of Financial Services or any other regulator. <Cite ids="src-orbit-circle-arc-mainnet" /> It also claims that USDC accounts for 98.8% of agent-driven transaction volume, linking a public Dune query of x402 volume by token. The release gives no method or date range (as of 2026-09-16). <Cite ids="src-orbit-circle-arc-mainnet src-orbit-circle-dune-x402-token" />
