Stripe launched Shared Payment Tokens with [ACP](/p/openai-acp/) on 2025-09-29 and the Agentic Commerce Suite on 2025-12-11. <Cite ids="src-stripe-acp-launch src-stripe-acs-launch" /> An SPT carries `usage_limits` (`currency`, `max_amount`, `expires_at`) and `seller_details`, and the seller confirms a PaymentIntent with it; OpenAI describes SPT as the first implementation compatible with the ACP Delegated Payment spec, but SPT itself is not part of the ACP spec. <Cite ids="src-stripe-spt src-openai-commerce-docs" /> Stripe's [MPP](/p/stripe-mpp/) integration accepts cards through SPTs, its machine payments preview added [x402](/p/x402/) on Base (reported, February 2026), and Stripe is a listed [UCP](/p/ucp/) co-developer. <Cite ids="src-stripe-mpp-docs src-x402-stripe-coverage src-ucp-site" />
