Tempo's mainnet (chain ID 4217) went live on 2026-03-18, and the same post introduced [MPP](/p/stripe-mpp/) as an open standard co-authored by Stripe and Tempo that supports stablecoins, cards and other methods, so Tempo is one MPP payment method among several rather than a requirement. <Cite ids="src-orbit-tempo-mainnet" /> Tempo Labs and Stripe maintain the MPP specifications repository, and the Apache-2.0 node software `tempoxyz/tempo` had release 1.16.0 on 2026-10-06. <Cite ids="src-orbit-mpp-specs-repo src-orbit-tempo-repo" /> Coverage dates the project announcement to 2025-09-04 and the public testnet to 2025-12-09 (reported). <Cite ids="src-orbit-tempo-testnet-theblock" />
