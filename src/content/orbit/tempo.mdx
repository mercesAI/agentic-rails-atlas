Tempo was announced on 2025-09-04 as a payments-first blockchain incubated by Stripe and Paradigm, and its public testnet went live on 2025-12-09. <Cite ids="src-orbit-tempo-paradigm src-orbit-tempo-testnet" /> Tempo's mainnet went live on 2026-03-18, and the same post introduced [MPP](/p/stripe-mpp/) as an open standard co-authored by Stripe and Tempo that supports stablecoins, cards and other methods, so Tempo is one MPP payment method among several rather than a requirement. <Cite ids="src-orbit-tempo-mainnet" /> The mainnet's chain ID is 4217. <Cite ids="src-stripe-mpp-tempo-connection" /> Tempo Labs and Stripe maintain the MPP specifications repository, and the node software `tempoxyz/tempo`, licensed under MIT or Apache-2.0 at the user's option, had release v1.16.1 on 2026-10-14. <Cite ids="src-orbit-mpp-specs-repo src-orbit-tempo-repo" />
