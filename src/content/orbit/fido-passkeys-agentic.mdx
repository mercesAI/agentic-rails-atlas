## What it is

A passkey is a sign-in credential based on FIDO standards, kept on a phone, a computer or a hardware security key. Passkeys use FIDO2: WebAuthn is the browser API and CTAP is the protocol between the device and an authenticator. Synced passkeys move across a user's devices through a passkey provider, while device-bound passkeys stay on one device. <Cite ids="src-orbit-fido-passkeys" /> WebAuthn Level 3 became a W3C Recommendation on 2026-08-25, and the newest CTAP is version 2.3, a FIDO Proposed Standard of 2026-02-26 (CTAP 2.2 dates from 2025-07-14). <Cite ids="src-gloss-webauthn-3 src-orbit-fido-specs-download" />

## The agentic working groups

On 2026-04-28 the FIDO Alliance announced a newly formed Agentic Authentication technical working group, chaired by members from CVS Health, Google and OpenAI, with vice-chairs from Amazon, Google and Okta. Its scope is how users delegate to agents with phishing-resistant authentication and how a service tells user-initiated actions from agent-initiated ones. The same release names a Payments technical working group chaired by Mastercard and Visa, and three focus areas: verifiable user instructions, agent authentication and trusted delegation for commerce. No deliverables or dates were given. <Cite ids="src-fido-agentic" />

Only the Agentic Authentication group was new that day. FIDO had launched a Payments working group on 2025-04-29, co-chaired by Visa and Mastercard. <Cite ids="src-fido-pwg" /> No source says whether the 2026 "Payments technical working group" is the same body under a new name. FIDO posts working and review drafts of other specifications on its download page, but no public FIDO draft or repository on agents was found there, on its agentic AI pages, in its news or in its GitHub organization on 2026-10-21. <Cite ids="src-fido-agentic src-ap2-fido-spec-download src-ap2-fido-agentic-ai" /> At a W3C and GS1 workshop on 2026-09-08 the Payments Technical Working Group presented targets: AP2 and Verifiable Intent specifications in a public repository for review in September 2026, harmonized specifications by the end of 2026 and a first consolidated version in mid-2027. These are stated targets, not deliverables. <Cite ids="src-ap2-fido-w3c-deck" />

## How it relates to the eight

On 2026-04-28 Google contributed [AP2](/p/ap2/) and Mastercard contributed Verifiable Intent, which Mastercard co-developed with Google to work with AP2. FIDO is now the home of both. <Cite ids="src-fido-agentic src-ap2-google-fido-blog" /> Passkeys also appear in the card networks' agent programs. Visa Intelligent Commerce authenticates payment instructions with passkeys, and Visa says every Agentic Ready transaction in Europe used Visa Payment Passkeys. These passkeys sit beside [Visa TAP](/p/visa-tap/), not inside its signature scheme. <Cite ids="src-visa-vic-dev src-visa-eu-live" /> Mastercard says its Agentic Tokens build on the same tokenization behind Payment Passkeys, and its live regional purchases for [Mastercard Agent Pay](/p/mastercard-agent-pay/) use Mastercard Payment Passkeys to authenticate consumers. <Cite ids="src-mcap-launch-2025 src-mcap-latam-2026" /> OpenAI co-chairs the agentic group, but [ACP](/p/openai-acp/) has no specification link to FIDO. No relation was found for UCP, x402 or ERC-8004. <Cite ids="src-fido-agentic" /> [MPP](/p/stripe-mpp/) has no FIDO working-group link either, but as of 2026-10-21 its Tempo methods accept WebAuthn (passkey) keys, for example as the access key that authorizes subscription renewals, and its Solana session draft allows passkey-backed voucher signers. <Cite ids="src-stripe-mpp-tempo-charge src-stripe-mpp-tempo-subscription src-orbit-mpp-solana-session" />
