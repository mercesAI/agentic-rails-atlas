Shopify announced Agentic Storefronts and an Agentic plan on 2026-01-11 alongside [UCP](/p/ucp/), which it co-develops; the channel surfaces Shopify Catalog data in AI assistants and got its own admin page on 2026-05-11. <Cite ids="src-orbit-shopify-news src-ucp-site src-orbit-shopify-changelog" /> As of 2026-11-14 Shopify's Help Center lists four AI channels, ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and Meta, each active by default for eligible stores; Meta joined the admin on 2026-09-08. <Cite ids="src-ucp-shopify-help-agentic-storefronts src-ucp-shopify-changelog-meta" /> Every channel needs products eligible for Shopify Catalog and completed store policies, and each sets its own country rules. <Cite ids="src-ucp-shopify-help-agentic-storefronts" />

Three channels offer a Shopify-powered direct checkout that is on by default, with an opt-out in the admin. Google AI Mode and Gemini needs a Google Merchant Center account and serves customers in the United States, Canada or Australia from stores based in any country, rolling out gradually. On 2026-10-20 the same page required a US-based store selling to US customers. <Cite ids="src-ucp-shopify-help-google" /> Google announced Canadian checkout on 2026-11-04; for Australia, Google's Merchant Center help scopes UCP-powered checkout to products eligible there, but no Google source says Australian checkout is live (read 2026-11-21). <Cite ids="src-ucp-google-canada src-ucp-merchant-help-ucp-checkout" /> Microsoft Copilot serves US customers, and Meta, on Meta surfaces including Muse when available, serves customers in Australia, Brazil, Canada, India, Japan, Mexico, New Zealand, Singapore, South Korea, the United Kingdom and the United States. <Cite ids="src-ucp-shopify-help-microsoft src-ucp-shopify-help-meta" /> ChatGPT is a discovery-focused referrer: buyers complete the purchase in the store's own checkout, in an in-app browser or a new tab, so there is no direct checkout setting. <Cite ids="src-ucp-shopify-help-chatgpt" /> Each channel page says there are no fees beyond standard payment processing fees. <Cite ids="src-ucp-shopify-help-google src-ucp-shopify-help-microsoft src-ucp-shopify-help-meta src-ucp-shopify-help-chatgpt" /> Secondary reports put the first, US-only Google enrollment at about 2026-09-18 to 2026-09-22 (reported), and Microsoft says Shopify merchants were enrolled in Copilot Checkout automatically, with controls in the Shopify admin. <Cite ids="src-ucp-ppcland-shopify src-ucp-microsoft-page" />

Beside the admin channel, Shopify offers agent-side access to the same catalog and checkout. Shop MCP (2026-10-13) gives an agent Catalog search, carts and Shop Pay checkout through one MCP connection at `https://mcp.shop.com`. The Fall '26 Edition (2026-10-28) adds Shop.dev, one connection to Catalog, sign-in with Shop, cart, checkout and Shop Pay for any agent or app with no key required, plus Universal Checkout across brands and WebMCP tools on every Liquid and Hydrogen storefront and checkout. <Cite ids="src-ucp-shopify-dev-changelog src-ucp-shopify-fall26-developer" /> These are developer products, not settings of the channel, and the developer post names neither ACP nor UCP. <Cite ids="src-ucp-shopify-fall26-developer" /> Shop Pay now works inside personal AI agents, starting with Muse, Claude Duet and Instinct, and Claude Duet, Grok Bot and Instinct also offer it for brands not on Shopify. For those off-Shopify agent purchases Shopify says Shop Pay uses tokens from Mastercard, Visa and Discover, with American Express coming soon; it names no network program. <Cite ids="src-ucp-shopify-fall26-merchant" /> A knowledge base page in Sales channels > Agentic, built into the admin on 2026-10-26, lets merchants review and publish the store facts that agents receive. <Cite ids="src-ucp-shopify-changelog-knowledge-base src-ucp-shopify-fall26-merchant src-ucp-shopify-help-agentic-storefronts" />

On the protocol side, Shopify's developer changelog puts UCP over MCP at `https://{shop-domain}/api/ucp/mcp`, and Shopify said on 2026-11-06 that UCP's Ask capability is live across its storefronts under the 2026-08-25 profile. It is retiring the Storefront MCP tool `search_shop_policies_and_faqs` in favor of UCP's `ask_business` by 2027-01-15. <Cite ids="src-ucp-shopify-dev-ask-deprecation src-ucp-stc-minutes-20261106" /> Sierra says every Shopify store also publishes a [Personal Agent Protocol](/orbit/personal-agent-protocol/) file and that Shopify co-authored that protocol's commerce extension, which hands the shopping to UCP (2026-11-13). <Cite ids="src-ucp-sierra-pap-1-0" /> OpenAI's 2026-03-24 post described Shopify catalog data reaching ChatGPT. <Cite ids="src-openai-product-discovery" />
