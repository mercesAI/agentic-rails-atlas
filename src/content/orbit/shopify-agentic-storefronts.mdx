Shopify announced Agentic Storefronts and an Agentic plan on 2026-01-11 alongside [UCP](/p/ucp/), which it co-develops; the channel surfaces Shopify Catalog data in ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Mode and Gemini. <Cite ids="src-orbit-shopify-news src-ucp-site" /> Shopify's Help Center says checkout is active by default for eligible US stores, while the Copilot, AI Mode and Gemini storefronts are in early access, and the channel got its own admin page on 2026-05-11. <Cite ids="src-orbit-shopify-help-requirements src-orbit-shopify-help-cx src-orbit-shopify-changelog" /> Secondary reports say eligible US merchants were enrolled in Google AI Mode checkout from about 2026-09-18 to 2026-09-22 with an opt-out (reported). <Cite ids="src-ucp-ppcland-shopify" /> OpenAI said on 2026-03-24 that Shopify catalog data reaches ChatGPT, with purchases completed on the merchant's store. <Cite ids="src-openai-product-discovery" />
