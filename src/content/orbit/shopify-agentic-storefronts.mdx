Shopify announced Agentic Storefronts and an Agentic plan on 2026-01-11 alongside [UCP](/p/ucp/), which it co-develops; the channel surfaces Shopify Catalog data in ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Mode and Gemini. <Cite ids="src-orbit-shopify-news src-ucp-site" /> Shopify's Help Center says checkout is active by default for eligible US stores, while the Copilot, AI Mode and Gemini storefronts are rolling out, and the channel got its own admin page on 2026-05-11. <Cite ids="src-orbit-shopify-help-requirements src-orbit-shopify-help-cx src-orbit-shopify-changelog" /> Its Google AI Mode and Gemini page says direct checkout is activated by default for eligible US stores, can be turned off, and carries no fees beyond standard payment processing. Microsoft says Shopify merchants were enrolled in Copilot Checkout automatically, with controls in the Shopify admin. <Cite ids="src-ucp-shopify-help-google src-ucp-microsoft-page" /> Secondary reports put the Google enrollment at about 2026-09-18 to 2026-09-22 (reported). <Cite ids="src-ucp-ppcland-shopify" /> OpenAI said on 2026-03-24 that Shopify catalog data reaches ChatGPT, with purchases completed on the merchant's store. <Cite ids="src-openai-product-discovery" />
