American Express announced the ACE Developer Kit on 2026-04-14 with five services: agent registration, account enablement, intent intelligence, payment credentials and cart context. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amex-ace-pr" /> Its program page, read on 2026-11-14, says the Account Enablement, Intent Intelligence and Payment Credentials specifications have been available since 2026-04-14, while the Agent Registration and Cart Context specifications are still under development. <Cite ids="src-scn-chatgpt-amex-agentic-page" /> On 2026-10-13 American Express published its Intent Intelligence framework. A Create Intent API captures the customer's purchase instructions and constraints, such as merchant and maximum budget; the customer authenticates and signs off, by passkey or through sign-off the issuer delegates to the agent or a wallet provider; then an Intent ID and a Proof of Intent Token are issued. <Cite ids="src-scn-chatgpt-amex-intent-release src-scn-chatgpt-amexio-intent" /> The token, a JSON Web Token, can be used to obtain a payment credential scoped to that intent; it expires on the date the customer confirmed and can be single-use or multi-use. <Cite ids="src-scn-chatgpt-amexio-intent" /> The release says American Express is engaging with EMVCo and supporting the x402 Foundation's work. <Cite ids="src-scn-chatgpt-amex-intent-release" />

OpenAI's 2026-11-13 post introducing [ChatGPT Wallet](/orbit/chatgpt-wallet/) quotes American Express's Luke Gebb calling Wallet the first ACE integration; American Express itself had published nothing on Wallet as of 2026-11-14, so that status rests on OpenAI's post (reported). <Cite ids="src-openai-chatgpt-wallet src-scn-chatgpt-amex-newsroom" /> Agent Purchase Protection stays forward-looking: the program page says American Express will in future protect eligible Card Members from charges caused by errors of registered agents that send it the Card Member's authenticated purchase intent, with a list of registered agents still to come, and neither company says Wallet purchases qualify. <Cite ids="src-scn-chatgpt-amex-agentic-page src-openai-chatgpt-wallet" /> American Express was named among [AP2](/p/ap2/) launch collaborators, is listed as a [UCP](/p/ucp/) endorser, and was a Premier member at the x402 Foundation's operational launch on 2026-07-14. <Cite ids="src-ap2-gcloud-launch src-ucp-site src-x402-lf-july" />
