American Express announced the ACE Developer Kit on 2026-04-14 with five services: agent registration, account enablement, intent intelligence, payment credentials and cart context. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amex-ace-pr" /> The same release introduced Agent Purchase Protection for eligible Card Members against charges caused by errors of registered AI agents, subject to terms, and the program page says only verified AI agents can transact; the wording is forward-looking. <Cite ids="src-orbit-amex-ace-pr src-orbit-amex-agentic-page" /> American Express was named among [AP2](/p/ap2/) launch collaborators, is listed as a [UCP](/p/ucp/) endorser, and was a Premier member at the x402 Foundation's operational launch on 2026-07-14. <Cite ids="src-ap2-gcloud-launch src-ucp-site src-x402-lf-july" />
