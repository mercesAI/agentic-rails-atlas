Microsoft announced Copilot Checkout on 2026-01-08 with PayPal, Shopify and Stripe, rolling out in the US; the merchant stays merchant of record and Copilot does not take that role. <Cite ids="src-ms-copilot-checkout src-ucp-microsoft-page" /> Microsoft's requirements page says Copilot Checkout requires a [UCP](/p/ucp/)-compliant feed (present tense) and that Microsoft Merchant Center "will support" UCP (future tense). <Cite ids="src-ucp-microsoft-page src-ms-agentic-commerce" /> Microsoft also says it is adopting open standards like [ACP](/p/openai-acp/), without documenting an ACP binding. <Cite ids="src-ms-copilot-checkout src-ms-agentic-commerce" />
