Microsoft announced Copilot Checkout on 2026-01-08 with partner activation across PayPal, Shopify and Stripe, rolling out in the US; the merchant stays merchant of record and Copilot does not take that role. <Cite ids="src-ms-copilot-checkout src-ucp-microsoft-page" /> As of 2026-11-14 Microsoft's requirements page lists only Shopify, whose merchants were enrolled automatically, and Stripe's Agentic Commerce Suite with Shared Payment Tokens as checkout partners; PayPal no longer appears. The page names Copilot.com and the Copilot mobile app as surfaces and limits eligibility to English-language merchants selling to US buyers in USD. <Cite ids="src-ucp-microsoft-page" /> On 2026-10-20 the same page said that Microsoft took no commission or affiliate fee today. That answer was removed by about 2026-11-06, and the page now makes no fee statement, which is not evidence that Microsoft charges one. <Cite ids="src-ucp-microsoft-page" /> Shopify's own Copilot channel page says its merchants pay no fees beyond standard payment processing. <Cite ids="src-ucp-shopify-help-microsoft" /> Microsoft's requirements page says Copilot Checkout requires a [UCP](/p/ucp/)-compliant feed (present tense) and that Microsoft Merchant Center "will support" UCP (future tense). <Cite ids="src-ucp-microsoft-page src-ms-agentic-commerce" /> Microsoft's launch post also says it is adopting open standards like [ACP](/p/openai-acp/), without documenting an ACP binding. <Cite ids="src-ms-copilot-checkout" />
