x402r is a third-party, non-custodial refund and arbitration protocol for [x402](/p/x402/) payments; the x402 docs list it as a third-party extension, and its SDK `@x402r/sdk` was first published on npm on 2026-03-20 (version 0.2.1 at the time of writing). <Cite ids="src-x402-docs-devtools src-orbit-x402r-sdk" /> A402 is a separate paper (arXiv:2603.01179, 2026-03-01) that argues x402 does not make execution, payment and delivery atomic and proposes Atomic Service Channels with TEE-assisted adaptor signatures. <Cite ids="src-x402-arxiv-2603-01179" /> No A402 deployment was found.
